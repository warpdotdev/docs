# Refer a friend

Think Warp would be the ideal product for someone you know? Use our invite system to onboard your friends! Open the invite modal from the top-right menu.

![Opening the invite modal in Warp](<../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2021-07-14 at 11.16.29.png>)

There are two ways you can invite a friend:&#x20;

1. Send them an invite link. This will redirect them to our product download page.
2. Input their email address which we use to send them an email.

![Send an invite two ways.](<../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2021-07-14 at 11.26.32.png>)

**NB:** As Warp is in closed beta right now (July 2021), we only enable a limited number of invites per user. The number of invites remaining only decreases once someone actually uses your link / invite code to download app. If you run out invites, you can always request more! We will respond to your request within 1 business day.

![Request more invites.](<../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2021-07-14 at 11.36.23.png>)
