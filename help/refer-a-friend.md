# Refer a Friend

Think Warp would be the ideal product for someone you know? You can invite your team or friends within the app! Open the invite modal from the top-right menu.

![Refer a friend](../.gitbook/assets/refer\_a\_friend.gif)

There are two ways you can invite a friend:

1. Send them an invite link. This will redirect them to our product download page.
2. Input their email address which we use to send them an email.

## Reward system

\[Only Available from 04/01/2022 onwards]

To thank you for spreading the word on Warp, we’ll be giving out these rewards:&#x20;

1. **1 referral**: You and your friend get an exclusive theme
2. **10 referrals**: Warp T-shirt
3. **20 referrals**: An invite to an insiders' program (private news and early access to features)

Make sure that your friend uses your personalized invite link to sign up for Warp so you can earn the rewards.&#x20;

### How do I claim the theme if I referred someone?

Once someone has signed up using your referral code, your theme should be automatically added to your [theme picker ](../features/themes.md)the next time you use Warp.&#x20;

### How do I claim the theme if I claimed a referral?

Once you log in to Warp for the first time, your theme should be available in your theme picker.

### How to claim the T-shirt if I've referred 10 people?

In the future, there will be an option for you to claim the shirt from within the app itself. But for now, you will receive an email to fill out a Typeform with your mailing address. We will mail you the T-shirt in your chosen size.&#x20;

If you have referred 10 people but have not received an email yet, please contact us at [hello@warp.dev](mailto:hello@warp.dev). For users who joined pre-public beta, we will send you this email shortly after the week of the public beta.&#x20;

### How to join the insiders' program?

You will receive a personal email invite to join the program.&#x20;

If you have referred 20 people but have not received an email yet, please contact us at [hello@warp.dev](mailto:hello@warp.dev). For users who joined pre-public beta, we will send you this email shortly after the week of the public beta.
