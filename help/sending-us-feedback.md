# Sending us Feedback

We would love to hear your feedback, bugs, and blockers to using Warp.

You can send us feedback:

* Using our [in-app feedback form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeaGI4rVr7eW3gOL1O3W9TDDOFhv49cMWYHN31ixgju7a3uUg/viewform): navigate to the app menu -> Send Feedback -> Open Feedback Form
* By [joining our Discord server](https://discord.gg/T2p5xFgpjr). Send us a message in `#bugs` and `#feature-requests`!
