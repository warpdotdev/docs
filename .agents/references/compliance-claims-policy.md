# Compliance claims policy

Apply this policy when writing, reviewing, or answering questions from Warp documentation and other customer-facing content, including content about Oz and the Automation Platform.

Warp is not HIPAA compliant. Omit HIPAA from customer-facing content and never imply that Warp or Oz supports HIPAA-regulated workloads or offers a HIPAA business associate agreement.

Include other certifications, attestations, or compliance commitments only when a current Warp Security or Legal-approved source confirms the exact claim and its scope. Omit unsupported claims and flag them internally.

Before retaining or adding a claim:

* Record the approved source, its date or revision, the approving Security or Legal authority, and the exact product, plan, provider, model, deployment, and data scope it covers in the PR evidence.
* Distinguish an attestation from a certification, provider-side retention from Warp storage, and technical controls from contractual commitments. Do not infer compliance from encryption, self-hosting, a provider's certification, or a telemetry setting.
* Do not treat existing docs, source code, a generated draft, a positioning document, a sales or support statement, or a general PR approval as Security or Legal approval.
* Omit a claim when its approval, currency, or scope cannot be established. Report the omitted claim and missing evidence in the internal handoff or PR's `Unverified claims` section, without exposing confidential source material. Do not leave the unsupported promise in public prose with a `VERIFY` marker.

For review, request changes if customer-facing content violates this policy. An ordinary documentation-risk waiver does not authorize a compliance claim.

This versioned policy is durable repository guidance, not a write to platform-managed Agent Memory.
