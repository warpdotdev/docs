# Support and Community

Find help when you need it, manage your plan, understand how Warp handles your data, and connect with other developers.

* [**Plans and billing**](plans-and-billing/plans-and-pricing.md) - Explore pricing plans, manage AI credits, configure your own API keys, and find answers to billing questions.
