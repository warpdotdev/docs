# Glboal Hotkey Window (Quake Mode)

Enable Quake Mode, by navigating to the Features section in the Settings Dialog.

After you've registered a keybinding you can show and hide Warp regardless of whether the app is focused.

You could customize the window’s pinned position and its width and height ratio relative to your active screen size. *Note:* Your new customization will apply the **next** time the a hotkey window is created and not the currently opened one.

If the window does not open after pressing the registered hotkey, check under System Preferences > Security & Privacy > Accessibility and tick the checkbox for Warp to grant access.
