# Themes

## What is it

Warp includes a number of themes (out-of-box) and also supports setting custom themes.

## How to access it

The Theme Picker can be accessed through the [Command Palette](../features/command-palette.md) or by:

1. Navigating to the top-right section of the Warp window
1. Clicking the kebab-menu to open the drop-down menu
1. Clicking on `Settings > Appearance`
1. Clicking the Custom Themes (shaded) box
1. Upon selecting a theme, Warp's appearance will update accordingly.&#x20;
1. Press the checkmark to save the selection, or the X to revert.

_Note:_ This setting persists i.e. Warp will open with the same settings in the next session.

## OS Theme Sync

Warp supports synchronizing your theme with the OS’s light and dark themes. To enable this:

1. Open the `Settings > Appearance` dialog.
1. Click the toggle "Sync with OS".
1. You will then be able to select a specific theme for when the OS is in light mode and dark mode.
