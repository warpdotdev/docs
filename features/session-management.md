# Session Management

Warp manages sessions in two different ways:

1. Through [Launch Configurations](/features/launch-configurations.md) that support saving a configurations of windows, tabs, and panes to open later.
1. Through automatic Session Restoration, which automatically restores the window and tabs from your previous session (Settings > Features > Restore windows, tabs, and panes on startup). 

## Launch Configuration Palette

Press `CTRL-CMD-L` to open the Launch Configuration Pallette--where you can view, search, and open any of your saved launch configurations.

After pressing `CMD-P` to open the [Command Palette](/features/command-palette.md), type `#` to switch into the Launch Configuration mode.
