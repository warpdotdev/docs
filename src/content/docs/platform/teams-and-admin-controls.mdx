A Warp team groups people and resources so you can manage membership, access, and settings. Team members can share factories, environments, secrets, and Warp Drive content. Teams are available on self-serve and Enterprise plans.

On the {VARS.WARP_AUTOMATION_PLATFORM}, teams share configuration for agent workflows. For example, a team can use a shared environment and team-owned secrets for a scheduled repository check instead of relying on one developer's personal credentials.

Individual users can also start standalone cloud runs without a team. Membership, plan access, funding, and external authorization are separate requirements. Check [cloud run access and billing](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/) before configuring a workflow. For factory membership and controls, use [Warp Factories team administration](/factories/teams-and-admin-controls/).

## Team roles and administrative scope

A role defines what you can access and which actions you can take in Warp. Your effective permissions also depend on your plan, memberships, and applicable policies.

These human team roles govern membership and shared settings:

* **Team owner** - Owns a self-serve team and can transfer ownership. Each self-serve team has one owner.
* **Team admin** - Manages the team's membership and configurable settings. Additional admins depend on the plan and are not Enterprise-only.
* **Member** - Uses shared resources within the applicable policies. Membership alone does not grant administration.

Warp Agent plans support additional admins on Build, Max, Business, and Enterprise, but not Free. See [plans and pricing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/) for plan details. For factory-plan requirements, use [Warp Factories team administration](/factories/teams-and-admin-controls/#team-roles-and-factory-access).

An Enterprise workspace groups one or more teams under one account. Workspace owners and admins administer all of its teams. A team admin administers only their team. Workspace policies can lock team settings. See [Enterprise roles and permissions](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/).

Cloud agent accounts are execution accounts for automations, not human team roles. See [Cloud agent accounts](/platform/agents/) for configuration and API keys.

## Creating your first team

If you don't belong to a team, you can create one for standalone integrations without setting up a factory:

1. Sign in to the <a href={`${VARS.WEB_APP_URL}/integrations`}>Integrations page in the {VARS.WEB_APP}</a>.
2. In "Integrations require a team", click **Create team**.
3. Enter a name in "Team name" and click **Create team**.
4. Confirm the message says your named team was created. Review the available integration controls.

Creating a team does not enable integrations on an ineligible plan or fund runs. If the integration controls are unavailable, review [integration access requirements](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/#requirements-for-integrations) and [run funding](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/#usage-requirements).

## Joining a team

Ask a team owner or admin for an invitation. For an email invitation, use the address that received it. Ask an admin to replace an expired or misaddressed invitation.

1. Open the invitation link and sign up or sign in to Warp.
2. Confirm the team name and signed-in email address.
3. If the link requires domain verification, enter an address on an approved domain and click **Send Code**. Enter the emailed code in "Verification Code", then click **Join Team**. Otherwise, click **Join team**.
4. Confirm Warp reports that you've joined the intended team, or ask an admin to verify your email in the team's member list.

Enterprise workspace membership does not automatically join every team. Follow [Joining a workspace or team](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#joining-a-workspace-or-team) for Enterprise invitations and discovery.

## Finding your team ID

Cloud-provider trust policies and other team-scoped configuration require the ID of the intended team, not its workspace.

1. Open the [Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/). In an Enterprise workspace, under "Team admin", click the team name, then **Members**. For a self-serve team, click **Teams**.
2. Confirm the team name. Copy its ID from the URL:
   * In `/admin/workspace/teams/TEAM_UID/members`, copy the value between `/teams/` and `/members`.
   * In `/admin/TEAM_UID/teams`, copy the value immediately after `/admin/`.

`TEAM_UID` represents your team's ID. The final URL component usually names the section, not the team. A workspace-wide URL such as `/admin/workspace/privacy` does not identify a team. If you cannot open the Admin Panel, ask a team or workspace admin for the ID.

## Managing membership and admins

Team owners and admins use the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/). Members who cannot open it should ask the person who invited them or a known admin for help. If no admin can be identified, [contact support](mailto:support@warp.dev).

Before inviting someone to a paid per-seat plan, review [member billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/pricing-faqs/#what-counts-as-a-team-member-and-how-does-billing-work-for-members). Adding an active member adds a prorated seat charge. Enterprise terms follow the contract.

### Inviting by email

1. Open the Admin Panel and click **Teams**. Confirm the team name before inviting colleagues.
2. Under "Invite by Email", enter email addresses separated by commas or spaces, then click **Send**. Confirm the invitations are pending.

### Inviting by link

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Toggle on **Invite by Link**.
3. To restrict joining to approved email domains, enter a domain under "Restrict by domain", then click **Add**. Confirm the domain appears in the list. Repeat for any additional domains before sharing.
4. Click **Copy invite link** and share the link securely.

People signing in with an address outside the approved domains must verify an allowed address before joining.

To disable the link, toggle off **Invite by Link**. To replace it, click **Reset links**, then **Reset** in the confirmation. The old link no longer works.

### Promoting or demoting admins

You must be a team owner or admin to change team-admin roles. Your plan must support additional admins before you can promote a member.

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Use the controls shown in the member list:
   * In the member-menu layout, next to the person you want to promote or demote, click the three dots. Click **Promote to team admin** or **Demote team admin**.
   * In the editable "Role" column, click the member's role dropdown. Select **Team Admin** to promote them. To demote an admin, select **Factory Editor** or **Factory User** for the factory access they should retain. This removes team administration and assigns that team-wide factory role.
3. If you're demoting yourself, review the confirmation and click **Demote to member**.
4. Confirm the updated role. If you lost Admin Panel access, ask another admin to confirm it.

See [factory role capabilities](/factories/permissions/#roles-and-capabilities) before choosing access during demotion.

### Transferring team ownership

Only the current self-serve team owner can transfer ownership. After the transfer, you become a Member and lose admin access. If the new owner's email is not on a work domain, Warp disables team discoverability. Adding an admin does not transfer ownership.

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Next to the member who will become the owner, click the three dots, then click **Transfer ownership**. If the list uses an editable role column instead, click the member's role dropdown and select **Owner**.
3. Click **Transfer** in the confirmation.
4. Ask the new owner to confirm their Owner role.

If you still need admin access, ask the new owner or another admin to grant it separately, if your plan supports additional admins.

For Enterprise administration, follow [Creating teams and choosing visibility](/enterprise/team-management/teams/#creating-teams-and-choosing-visibility), [Removing a workspace member](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#removing-a-workspace-member), [Changing workspace roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#changing-workspace-roles), or [Transferring workspace ownership](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#transferring-workspace-ownership).

### Removing a member or leaving a team

You must be a team owner or admin to remove a member from a self-serve team. The owner must transfer ownership before they can be removed. Removal ends access to team resources and the team's usage balance. Purchased usage stays with the team.

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Next to the person you want to remove, click the three dots, then click **Remove from team**.
3. Check the email address and team in the confirmation. Click **Remove**.
4. Confirm the person no longer appears in the member list.

To leave a team from the browser, ask another admin to remove your membership. See [usage when team membership changes](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#when-team-membership-changes) for balance access and per-seat billing consequences.

## Configuring shared controls

In the Admin Panel, confirm the team or workspace before opening the relevant section. The plan determines which controls are available. Workspace policies can restrict team admins.

### GitHub authorization

Under **Platform**, **Enabled GitHub Orgs** associates the team with GitHub App installations for supported standalone workflows. The app installation's repository selection is configured in GitHub, not by team membership alone.

Follow [Team GitHub authorization](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/#team-github-authorization) for setup. Personal API keys, agent API keys, and integration-triggered runs use different identities. Do not apply one run's repository-access or billing rule to every trigger.

### Shared credentials and models

Use [team secrets](/platform/secrets/#team-secrets) for standalone workflows that must not depend on an individual's secret. A secret's ownership and the run's configured secret grants are separate.

Use **Models** for [team-managed provider keys and endpoints](/enterprise/enterprise-features/team-managed-keys-and-endpoints/) on eligible plans. Shared credentials are not a model restriction. Enterprise [model policies](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/#models-settings) govern enforced model availability.

### Artifacts and privacy

Review the [sharing and security implications](/agents/capabilities/computer-use/artifacts-in-prs/) before changing how agents attach screenshots and videos. In the Admin Panel, open **Platform**. Click the "Agent artifact attachments" dropdown and select **Disabled**, **Link only**, or **Embed**.

For workspace collection policies, use the [Enterprise Admin Panel](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/#privacy-settings). See [Privacy and data control](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/) for retention and storage. Disabling personal Terminal conversation sync does not disable cloud-run storage.

## Usage and run visibility

Use [cloud run access and billing](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/#billing-for-cloud-agent-runs) to determine the user or team billed for a standalone run. Schedules and agent API keys can draw from different balances. Check the balance for the billed account rather than assuming all team workflows use one personal allowance.

Use the [cloud agent management surfaces](/platform/managing-cloud-agents/) to inspect runs. Enterprise workspace admins can manage team-owned runs across teams. That authority does not automatically grant access to personally owned runs. See [workspace run visibility](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#cloud-agent-run-visibility).
