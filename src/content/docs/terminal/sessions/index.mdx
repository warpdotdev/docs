1. [Launch Configurations](/sessions/launch-configurations/) supports saving a configuration of windows, tabs, and panes to open later.
2. [Session Navigation](/sessions/session-navigation/) enables you to easily navigate to any session in Warp.
3. [Session Restoration](/sessions/session-restoration/) automatically restores the window and tabs from your previous session.
