Connect a factory to Microsoft Teams so mentions in standard and shared channels start work and receive replies in the same thread.

## Who completes each part

* **Warp workspace admin** - Starts the connection or sends a setup request.
* **Teams approver** - Uploads, approves, and installs the Warp app under your organization's Teams policies, then connects the installation to Warp. Different people can handle app approval and adding the app to a team.
* **Factory editor** - Selects the connected teams and channels for the factory.

:::caution
The integration supports standard and shared channels. Private channels and direct messages are not supported.
:::

## Set up Microsoft Teams

1. **Open Microsoft Teams setup.** In the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a>, choose **Microsoft Teams** during factory creation or from the factory's **Settings**. If it isn't available, <a href={VARS.CONTACT_SALES_URL}>contact sales</a>.
2. **Choose who completes setup.** Continue yourself if your Teams permissions allow it. Otherwise, [send a setup request](/factories/connect-your-factory/#request-setup-from-an-administrator) to someone who can approve and install the app under your organization's policies.
3. **Download the Warp app package.** Sign in with Microsoft and download `warp-microsoft-teams-app.zip`. It contains the app manifest and icons, with no executable files. A delegated recipient uses the approval link without your Warp session.
4. **Upload and approve the app.** In the [Teams admin center](https://admin.teams.microsoft.com), go to **Teams apps** > **Manage apps**, click **Upload new app**, then upload and approve the package.
5. **Add Warp to each team.** In Microsoft Teams, open **Apps** > **Built for your org** > **Warp**, then add Warp to each team the factory should use.
6. **Connect the installed team to the Warp workspace.** Back in setup, select and connect the team. A delegated recipient completes this step from the approval link.
7. **Select teams and channels for the factory.** During factory creation or in the factory's **Settings**, an editor chooses **Microsoft Teams**, selects the connected teams and at least one standard channel in each, then clicks **Enable**.

Warp creates an enabled **teams-app-mentions** automation for the teams and channels selected in the last step.

The app installation connects Teams to the workspace; the requester still selects teams and channels for the factory. Teams app policies determine who can upload, approve, or add the app. This setup isn't the Microsoft Entra consent flow used for Azure DevOps.

## Test the connection

1. In one of the selected standard channels, mention **@Warp** with a small request.
2. If Warp prompts you to link accounts, follow the private sign-in link. The Microsoft account must map to an active member of the factory's Warp workspace. Then send the mention again.
3. Confirm that Warp replies in the same thread and that a work item appears in the factory [dashboard](/factories/factory-dashboard/).

## Start and continue work

* **Start work** - Mention **@Warp** in a configured channel or thread. The factory includes supported attachments and replies in the same thread. Unsupported attachments don't block the request text.
* **Continue work** - Reply in a thread that already has a factory work item. When responses to thread replies are enabled, eligible replies continue that work without another mention.
* **Use shared channels** - Root messages require **@Warp**. See the [shared-channel rules](#use-shared-channels).
* **Review results** - Follow progress and results in Teams. Code changes can also open a pull request.

Automation-started work runs as the factory agent selected in that automation. To manage your account link, mention **@Warp** with `sign in`, `sign out`, or `help` in a channel where the app is installed. For `sign in`, follow the private link, then send your request again. `sign out` unlinks your account from every factory in that Microsoft organization.

## Choose what starts work

Connect Microsoft Teams in the UI, then edit the seeded **teams-app-mentions** automation. Its **App mentioned** trigger starts work only when someone mentions **@Warp**, the default for general-purpose channels.

Use **Message posted in channel** when every matching message in a dedicated standard channel should start work without a mention. In the automation editor, choose exactly one team and at least one standard channel. You can narrow either event with keywords; definition files can also filter by author.

An app mention is also a posted channel message. If both triggers target the same channel, one mention can start two runs. Narrow or remove one trigger to prevent duplicates.

## Use shared channels

A shared channel belongs to an owning Teams team in one Microsoft 365 organization. These rules apply:

* **Install location** - Add the Warp app to the team that owns the shared channel, then connect that installation to the Warp workspace.
* **Eligible users** - Only people in the installation's Microsoft 365 organization with a Microsoft account linked to an active Warp workspace member can start or continue work. Guests and people from other organizations receive a private notice.
* **New work** - A new root message must mention **@Warp**.
* **Thread replies** - Eligible replies can continue an existing factory work item when responses to thread replies are enabled.
* **History** - An agent run can read only its originating shared-channel thread. Warp checks the originating sender's current Teams account binding before reading it.

The UI channel picker lists standard channels only. To use a shared channel, add its channel ID in the factory definition as described in [Configure Microsoft Teams as code](#configure-microsoft-teams-as-code).

## What agents can do in Teams

Connecting Microsoft Teams gives factory agents Warp-managed Teams tools. An agent can:

* Read a standard-channel thread or the shared-channel thread that started its run.
* Reply in a thread.
* Post a new top-level channel message.
* Resolve a Teams member.

These tools are separate from [configured MCP servers](/platform/mcp/). They cannot list channels, read private channels, send direct messages, add reactions, upload files, or read other shared-channel threads.

## Configure Microsoft Teams as code

Prefer the UI setup above, then edit the seeded **teams-app-mentions** automation. The seeded file already contains the correct team and channel IDs.

```yaml title="factory.yaml"
integrations:
  - type: microsoft-teams
    microsoft-teams:
      autoRespondToThreadReplies: false
```

Omit `autoRespondToThreadReplies`, or set it to `true`, for the default-on behavior (eligible plain replies in an existing factory thread can continue work without another mention).

```markdown title="automations/teams-app-mentions/automation.md"
---
enabled: true
agent: foreman
triggers:
  - provider: teams
    event: app_mention
    filter:
      team_ids: [MICROSOFT_GRAPH_TEAM_UUID]
      channel_ids: [19:example@thread.tacv2]
---

Triage the Teams request and continue in the same thread.
```

The two events have these definition-file values:

* **App mentioned** - `app_mention`
* **Message posted in channel** - `message_posted`

Each trigger requires one Microsoft Graph team UUID in `team_ids` and one or more Bot Framework channel conversation IDs in `channel_ids`. Channel IDs use the `19:…@thread.tacv2` form. These filters are inclusion lists only.

For UI-selected teams and standard channels, copy these values from the seeded automation. You can also read them from the factory Microsoft Teams list-teams and list-channels API response fields named `id`. For a shared channel, copy its channel conversation ID from the Teams link or Microsoft Graph, then add it to `channel_ids`.

Optional `keywords` and `user_ids` filters narrow matching by message content and author.

See the [Factory definition syntax](/factories/factory-as-code/#integrations) for integration settings, triggers, and filter shapes.

## Troubleshooting

* **Microsoft Teams is missing in Warp** - Ask a workspace admin to confirm the integration is enabled. If it isn't available, <a href={VARS.CONTACT_SALES_URL}>contact sales</a>.
* **An installed team is missing in Warp** - Confirm the app is approved and added to that team in Microsoft Teams, then return to setup to connect the installation to Warp. For an inactive approval link, see [managing pending requests](/factories/connect-your-factory/#manage-pending-requests).
* **Warp says, "This Microsoft Teams channel isn't connected to a Warp Factory. Connect this team to a Factory, then mention this bot again."** - Finish connecting the installation to Warp, then select the team and channel in the factory's **Settings** and click **Enable**. Mention **@Warp** again.
* **Warp doesn't acknowledge a mention** - Confirm the team and channel are selected, mention **@Warp**, and follow any private account-linking prompt. For a shared channel, check the [shared-channel rules](#use-shared-channels).
* **A channel is missing from the picker** - The picker lists standard channels only. Add a shared channel through the [factory definition](#configure-microsoft-teams-as-code).
* **One mention starts two runs** - Narrow or remove overlapping **App mentioned** and **Message posted in channel** triggers.
* **An agent can't post to Teams** - Confirm the factory still includes the Microsoft Teams integration and that the app remains installed in the selected team.

### Disconnect Microsoft Teams

Remove Microsoft Teams from the factory's **Settings** or definition to stop that factory from using the integration. Uninstalling the Warp app from a Teams team stops new activity from that team.

## Privacy

The factory's app uses message content and supported attachments from the channels you configure, and from threads it continues there, to run factory work. Linking your Microsoft account maps you to your Warp account. See the [Warp Privacy Policy](https://www.warp.dev/privacy).
