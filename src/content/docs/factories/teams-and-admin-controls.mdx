A Warp team groups people and resources so you can manage membership, access, and settings together. Team members can share factories, environments, secrets, and Warp Drive content. Teams are available on self-serve and Enterprise plans.

Factory access and team administration use different roles. A team member with the Editor factory role can configure a factory, but that role does not grant permission to manage team membership or shared team settings.

## Team roles and factory access

<span id="what-is-a-team" />
<span id="teams-and-workspaces" />
<span id="permissions-overview" />
<span id="team-roles-and-permissions" />

Team roles determine who can manage membership and shared team settings. Their permissions also depend on your plan and workspace policies:

| Role | Administration scope |
| --- | --- |
| Team owner | The sole owner of a self-serve team. Can transfer ownership |
| Team admin | Manages the team's members and configurable settings. Cannot transfer ownership |
| Member | Uses shared resources within the applicable factory role and policies |

Teams on the Start and Scale plans each have one owner. The plans differ in whether they support additional team admins:

| Plan | Additional team admins | Factory access for non-admin members |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | Not included | Editor |
| Scale | The owner and existing admins can add admins | Editor |

An Enterprise workspace groups one or more teams under one account. The workspace owner and admins inherit Admin access to factories across every team, including teams they don't belong to. Team admins inherit Admin access only to their own team's factories. See [Enterprise roles and permissions](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/).

Factory user and Editor are factory-access roles, not team-administration roles. See [Managing factory permissions](/factories/permissions/) for their capabilities, Enterprise role assignment, defaults, and per-factory overrides. Account-specific access can differ. Ask your owner or [contact support](mailto:support@warp.dev) if your controls don't match the expected plan.

## Checking your role

1. In the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a>, click your profile photo or initials at the bottom of the left sidebar to open the account menu. Confirm the selected team.
2. Read your role badge: "Member," "Admin," or "Workspace admin." A self-serve owner's badge also shows "Admin," so the badge does not identify who owns the team.
3. If you have admin access, click **Admin panel**. Find your entry in the team's member list to confirm your exact role.

If you don't have admin access and need help confirming your role, ask the person who invited you or a known admin. The Admin Panel is not a member-accessible directory. If you cannot identify an admin, [contact support](mailto:support@warp.dev).

## Creating or joining a team

<span id="creating-a-team" />
<span id="joining-a-team" />

To create or join your first self-serve team:

1. Sign in to the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a>.
2. If a list of existing teams displays:
   * **Join a team** - Select an existing team.
   * **Create a new team** - Click **Create a new team**, if offered. Enter a "Team name".
3. Click **Build your first Factory** to create or join the team and start factory setup.

If no existing teams are listed, click **Build your first Factory** to create a team with an automatically generated name. If the page instead says "Contact an admin to join a team," ask an admin for an invitation.

If you create a team, you become its owner. The account menu's **New Team** control is for Enterprise workspace administration, not for self-serve team creation.

To join by invitation:

1. Open the invitation link in a browser. Sign in with the invited account.
2. Check the team name. Click **Join team**.
3. If requested, complete domain verification.
4. Return to the factory web app. Check the account menu for your team. Reload if needed.

Ask an admin to replace expired invitations or invitations with incorrect email addresses.

Before inviting colleagues, review how your plan bills membership and funds factory runs:

| Plan | Subscription and shared funding |
| --- | --- |
| Start | No subscription. Factory runs use purchased usage. |
| Scale | One team subscription rather than a subscription fee for each member. Factory runs draw from shared team usage. |
| Enterprise | Membership charges and usage funding follow your [contract](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/). |

Members' runs use shared funding, so additional activity can increase usage charges.

Team owners and admins can invite colleagues to a self-serve team from the browser-based Admin Panel:

### Inviting by email

1. Open the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) in a browser and click **Teams**. Confirm the team name at the top of the page before inviting colleagues.
2. Under **Invite by Email**, enter the addresses and click **Send**. Confirm the invitations are pending.

### Inviting by link

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Teams**. Confirm the team name at the top of the page.
2. Enable **Invite by Link** if needed.
3. To restrict joining by email domain, enter a domain under "Restrict by domain" and click **Add**. Confirm the domain is listed. People signing in with another domain must verify an address on an allowed domain before joining.
4. Copy the invitation link and share it through a secure channel.

On eligible self-serve teams, **Team Discovery** lets colleagues with the same email domain find and join the team without an invitation.

### Removing a member

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Teams** and find the member in the team's member list.
2. Open the member's actions menu and choose **Remove member**.
3. Confirm the intended person before completing removal.

Purchased usage stays with the team. Removing a member ends their access to that balance. See [usage when team membership changes](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#when-team-membership-changes).

For Enterprise workspace invitations, hidden teams, and removing someone from the entire workspace, follow [Managing Enterprise teams](/enterprise/team-management/teams/).

## Managing admins and ownership

<span id="multi-admin-support" />

### Promoting or demoting admins

Your plan must support additional admins. Buying usage does not enable additional admins. These steps change team administration, not factory access:

1. In the browser Admin Panel, open the team's member list.
2. Open the member's three-dots menu and click **Promote to team admin** or **Demote team admin**. If the list has an editable role column, click the member's role dropdown and select **Team Admin** to promote the member instead.
3. Complete any confirmation and check the updated role.

### Transferring team ownership

Only the current self-serve team owner can transfer ownership. After the transfer, you become a Member and lose admin access. If the new owner's email is not on a work domain, Warp disables team discoverability.

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Teams** and find the member who will become the owner.
2. Open their actions menu and click **Transfer ownership**. If the list has an editable role column, click the member's role dropdown and select **Owner** instead.
3. Click **Transfer** in the confirmation.
4. Ask the new owner to confirm their Owner role.

If you still need admin access, ask the new owner or another admin to grant it separately, if your plan supports additional admins.

Enterprise workspace ownership is separate. Follow [Changing workspace roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#changing-workspace-roles).

<span id="leaving-and-deleting-teams" />

### Leaving a team

If you own the team, transfer ownership before leaving or deleting your account. To leave a self-serve team from the browser, ask an admin to remove your membership. A self-serve team cannot be deleted while it has unused purchased usage. [Contact support](mailto:support@warp.dev) for help with that balance.

## Shared settings and usage

<span id="managing-a-team-without-warp-terminal" />

### Configuring shared settings

In the factory web app's account menu, admins click **Admin panel** to open team-wide settings. Confirm the selected team or workspace before changing a control.

* **Models** - Configure [team-managed keys and endpoints](/enterprise/enterprise-features/team-managed-keys-and-endpoints/) on eligible plans. Provider credentials and factory [harness authentication](/factories/harnesses/authentication/) are separate.
* **Privacy** - Review content collection and telemetry controls. Scale includes shared data controls that Start does not include.
* **Enterprise policies** - Workspace admins manage supported policies across teams or delegate them. Enterprise adds enforced secret redaction, a control restricting direct API inference to the US, and customer-hosted data storage. The inference control is not an all-data residency guarantee. See the [Enterprise Admin Panel](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/).

### Changing content collection

Available choices depend on your plan and workspace policies. Some plans require collection to remain **Disabled**, so you cannot change the dropdown or let members choose their own preference. Ask a workspace admin about workspace-enforced settings.

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Privacy**.
2. Click the "UGC Data Collection" dropdown and select **Disabled** to stop content collection for service improvement. To let members choose individually, select **Respect User Setting**, if available.
3. Reload the page and confirm your selection is saved.

Content collection, provider retention, and product storage are separate. The cloud conversation storage control applies to personal Terminal conversations. Turning it off does not stop Warp from storing factory runs. Follow [factory ZDR guidance](/factories/zero-data-retention/) and [self-hosted data storage](/factories/self-hosting/data-storage/) for factory-specific choices.

### Reviewing usage and billing

On the Scale plan, admins can see each member's usage totals with unlimited history. Members who aren't admins see only their own usage.

Use [Managing factory plans and billing](/factories/managing-plans-and-billing/) to check shared balances, request an upgrade, or open billing. Viewing usage, purchasing usage, changing payment details, and managing subscriptions are separate permissions.
