Give teammates **Factory user** access to run work without changing the factory, or **Editor** access to configure its workers, repositories, and automations.

Enterprise plans include Factory user and Editor role assignment. On Start and Scale plans, non-admin members have Editor access.

If the available role controls don't match your plan, confirm the selected team and plan with an admin or [contact support](mailto:support@warp.dev).

## Team-wide roles and factory-specific access

A team-wide factory role applies to every factory on the team. To override that role for an individual factory, assign the person a different role in that factory’s access settings.

For example, a teammate with Factory user access across the team can have Editor access to the payments factory they maintain.

For Enterprise team members who aren't admins, Warp uses the first role set in this list:

| Priority | Role source |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | The person's role assigned for this factory |
| 2 | The person's team-wide factory role |
| 3 | The applicable workspace or team default |
| 4 | Editor, if no role or default is set |

Defaults apply to new and existing members without an assigned role. A workspace default takes priority over the team default. Changing a default doesn't replace a person's assigned team-wide or factory-specific role.

## Roles and capabilities

| Action | Factory user | Editor | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View the factory and run work | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Edit the factory's name, handle, and definition | No | Yes | Yes |
| Configure workers, repositories, and automations | No | Yes | Yes |
| Attach existing secrets, MCP servers, and integrations | No | Yes | Yes |
| Manage factory roles | No | No | Yes |
| Create a factory | No | Yes | Yes |
| Delete the factory | No | No | Yes |

Permission to create factories depends on your team-wide factory role. Assigning someone Editor access to an individual factory does not let them create new factories.

Team and workspace admins and owners inherit Admin access within their team or workspace. You can’t grant or reduce that access through a factory’s role settings. See [team roles and factory access](/factories/teams-and-admin-controls/#team-roles-and-factory-access).

Editors can attach existing team secrets, MCP servers, and integrations to a factory. Connecting new team-wide integrations and managing shared runners require separate team or workspace permissions.

## Changing a team-wide role

You must be a team or workspace admin, and your account must include factory role management. These steps assign factory access to a non-admin team member. If you select a factory role for a team admin in the Admin Panel, you also demote them to a member. Follow [Changing team admins](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/#changing-team-admins) for Enterprise demotion or [self-serve admin management](/factories/teams-and-admin-controls/#promoting-or-demoting-admins).

1. In the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a> sidebar, expand the factory and click **Settings**.
2. Under **Settings**, click **Access**, then click **Manage team access**.
3. In the Admin Panel, confirm the team name. Find the teammate in the member list.
4. In the "Role" column, click the teammate's role dropdown and select **Factory User** or **Factory Editor**. The selection saves immediately.
5. Wait for the update to complete. Reload the page and confirm the role persists.

To open the member list directly, sign in to the [Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/). For a self-serve team, click **Teams**. In an Enterprise workspace, under "Team admin", click the team name, then **Members**. Confirm the team name before assigning a role.

The role applies across the team's factories, except where the teammate has a factory-specific role. The Admin Panel's **Factory Editor** and **Factory User** options correspond to Editor and Factory user access.

## Changing the default factory role

You must be a team or workspace admin, and your account must include factory role management. The default affects new and existing members without an assigned role. It does not replace explicit assignments or inherited Admin access.

1. Open the team's member list in the Admin Panel using [the route above](#changing-a-team-wide-role). Confirm the team name.
2. Under "Default Factory role", click the dropdown and select **Editor** or **Factory user**.
3. Wait for the update to complete. Reload the page and confirm the selected default persists.

If a workspace default controls the team's default, ask a workspace admin to review it. If the role controls are missing, confirm your plan and access with an admin or [contact support](mailto:support@warp.dev).

## Changing a role for one factory

Team and workspace admins can change a teammate's role for one factory:

1. In the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a> sidebar, expand the factory and click **Settings**.
2. Under **Settings**, click **Access**.
3. Find the teammate by name or email. Click their role dropdown and select **Factory user** or **Editor**.
4. Click **Save changes**.

Reload the factory settings and confirm the role persists. The change applies only to this factory. The teammate keeps their existing role in other factories.

## Factory permissions and external access

Factory roles don’t grant access to repositories or external services. A run’s access to those resources depends on its configured [repository identity](/factories/factory-as-code/#credentialstrategy), [secrets](/factories/secrets/), and [MCP servers](/factories/factory-as-code/).

Factory roles don't determine who can approve specifications or merge pull requests. Configure [human decisions in the Blueprint](/factories/blueprints/#gates-ask-for-answers-not-automatic-approval). Repository permissions and branch protection govern pull request approval and merging.
