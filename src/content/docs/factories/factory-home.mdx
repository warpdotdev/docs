The **Home** page in the {VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP} brings together your factory runs and items that need your attention. By default, the **Waiting on you** status filter is selected, so you can answer questions, approve specs, and review pull requests without checking each factory's **Runs** page.

Open the <a href={`${VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}/home`}>Home page</a> or click **Home** in the web app sidebar.

<span id="what-appears-in-your-inbox" />

## What appears in Waiting on you

The view groups pending items by work item across your factories. Items include:

* **Asked question** - An agent needs a decision from you before it can continue.
* **Answered question** - An agent answered your question. The answer is for your information; no action is required.
* **Spec review** - A spec needs your approval before implementation starts.
* **PR review** - One or more pull requests are ready for your review.
* **Blocked** - A run stopped and needs attention, and nothing else on that work item already needs yours.
* **Failed** - A run ended in an error, and nothing else on that work item already needs your attention.

Home is personal, not a team-wide feed. Its run views show factory runs you started. **Waiting on you** shows items directed to you, which can also include a GitHub PR review requested from you on someone else's work.

Items clear when they stop being relevant. For example, a PR review clears when a person submits a review or the pull request is merged or closed, and a question clears when its answer is submitted. You can also mark an item as resolved yourself.

## Reviewing items waiting on you

1. Go to the **Home** page and click **Waiting on you** if another status is selected.
2. Click a row to open its conversation or details in a side pane. Use the linked pull request, spec, or originating thread or ticket to review the request in context. Where available, **View run details** opens the full run.
3. After handling an item, click **Mark as resolved** on the row or in the side pane. The item leaves **Waiting on you**. Click **Undo** in the confirmation to restore an item resolved by mistake.

Opening an item marks it as read without resolving it. To change read state yourself, select rows using their selection controls, or click **Select all**, then use **Mark as read** or **Mark as unread** in the selection menu. The same menu offers **Mark as resolved** for the selected items.

## Finding other runs on Home

Use **Active**, **Complete**, **Blocked**, or **Failed** to view your other factory runs. Click the selected status again to remove the status filter. A run with a pending item appears in **Waiting on you** rather than its other status view.

Use **Search your runs...** to narrow the list. Results are grouped under **Today**, **This week**, and **All time**. Resolving an attention item doesn't delete its run; use the other status views or the [Runs page](/factories/factory-dashboard/#inspect-runs) to find it.
