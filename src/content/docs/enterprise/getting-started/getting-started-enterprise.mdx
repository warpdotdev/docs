Set up Warp Enterprise for your organization: configure SSO, organize teams, invite colleagues, and apply workspace policies.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin:

* You have an active Warp Enterprise subscription
* You have admin access to your organization's identity provider (Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, etc.)
* You have workspace admin access, or a workspace owner or admin will perform the workspace-wide steps

## Step 1: Configure Single Sign-On (SSO)

Warp uses SSO to authenticate users in your Enterprise workspace. Warp supports Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, OneLogin, and SAML 2.0 or OIDC compatible providers through [WorkOS](https://workos.com).

Contact your account team or [Enterprise support](https://www.warp.dev/contact-sales) to coordinate SSO setup. Follow [Single Sign-On (SSO)](/enterprise/security-and-compliance/sso/) for the setup process and supported providers.

Before rolling out SSO, [test login in an incognito window](/enterprise/security-and-compliance/sso/#testing-sso).

:::note
After enabling SSO, existing users who signed up with email or OAuth will need to [link their accounts to SSO](https://app.warp.dev/link_sso) before they can continue using Warp with your organization. See [troubleshooting SSO account linking](/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/troubleshooting-login-issues/#i-logged-in-with-another-method-before-and-now-cant-use-sso) for details.
:::

## Step 2: Set up your workspace and teams

<span id="creating-your-team" />

### Initial Enterprise provisioning

Your Warp account team coordinates initial setup, including provisioning a team or applying the Enterprise plan to an existing team. Applying the Enterprise plan is not self-service. Contact your account manager to confirm the account and initial administrators.

A workspace groups your Enterprise teams under one account. Once the workspace is ready, workspace admins can [create additional teams and choose their visibility](/enterprise/team-management/teams/#creating-teams-and-choosing-visibility).

<span id="team-settings" />

### Workspace and team settings

Configure each choice at its intended scope:

* **Authentication and company domains** - Coordinate SSO and domain configuration with your account team. [Domain capture](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#workspace-settings-and-team-settings) adds users to the workspace. Workspace membership does not automatically join every team.
* **Team membership and visibility** - Use [Enterprise team management](/enterprise/team-management/teams/) to organize teams and choose how colleagues find and join them.
* **Shared policies** - Use the [Enterprise Admin Panel](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/) to enforce supported workspace values or delegate choices to teams.

## Step 3: Invite and manage users

### Inviting users

Review your [Enterprise membership and billing terms](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/) before inviting colleagues. Workspace admins can invite people to the workspace or a specific team. Team admins invite people to their own team.

Choose the invitation that matches the recipient's intended access:

* [Invite by email](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#inviting-members-by-email) to name specific recipients, including people joining hidden teams
* [Share a workspace invite link](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#sharing-a-workspace-invite-link) to let recipients select an open team
* [Share a team invite link](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#sharing-a-team-invite-link) for a specific open team

Recipients follow [Joining a workspace or team](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#joining-a-workspace-or-team). For a workspace invitation, if no open team is available, the recipient can join only the workspace. Add that person to a team before they start cloud agent or factory runs.

### User roles and permissions

Choose the level of administration each person needs:

* **Workspace owner or admin** - Manages workspace-wide membership and settings, and inherits administration access to its teams.
* **Team admin** - Manages the assigned team without automatically gaining workspace-wide authority.
* **Member** - Uses resources within the applicable team memberships, factory roles, and policies.

See [Roles and permissions](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/) for team roles and [Workspace roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#workspace-roles) for independent workspace administration.

### Managing admins

For team administration, follow [Promoting or demoting admins](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/#promoting-or-demoting-admins). For workspace-wide administration, follow [Changing workspace roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#changing-workspace-roles). Changing one role does not transfer the other.

## Step 4: Configure the Admin Panel

The Admin Panel has separate workspace and team scopes. Confirm the scope before changing a policy.

### Accessing the Admin Panel

1. Open the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) and sign in. Confirm the workspace name.
2. Under "Workspace admin" in the sidebar, open the section for a workspace-wide policy.
3. For a team setting, click the team's name under "Team admin", then open the relevant section.

<span id="key-admin-panel-sections" />

Follow the [Enterprise Admin Panel guidance](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/) for policy controls, model configuration, billing, and sharing. The team's **Models** page is under "Team admin", not the workspace sections.

### How settings enforcement works

For supported controls, a workspace admin can enforce a value or select **Respect Team Setting**. Team admins cannot override a workspace-enforced value. Delegated team controls remain subject to plan restrictions.

Some team controls offer **Respect User Setting** so members can choose their own preference. Not every control supports delegation. Follow [Enforcing or delegating workspace policies](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/#enforcing-or-delegating-workspace-policies) to apply a value and check its effect on a team.

## Step 5: Set up team resources

### Enable Codebase Context

Codebase Context indexes Git repositories for agent context. To enable it across the workspace:

1. In the Admin Panel, confirm the workspace. Under "Workspace admin", click **Code**.
2. Click the "Codebase Indexing" dropdown and select **Enabled**.
3. Reload the page and confirm the selected value persists.

To leave indexing under team control, select **Respect Team Setting** instead. See [Codebase Context](/agents/capabilities/codebase-context/) for repository indexing and limits.

### Create shared Warp Drive resources

Create Warp Drive resources your team can reuse:

* **Workflows** - Parameterized commands for common tasks (deployments, environment setup)
* **Notebooks** - Interactive runbooks for onboarding and operational procedures
* **Prompts** - Saved agent prompts for recurring tasks (code review, test generation)
* **Rules** - Coding standards and conventions that agents should follow
* **Environment Variables** - Shared configuration for development environments

See [Warp Drive documentation](/knowledge-and-collaboration/warp-drive/) for details on creating team resources.

### Configure MCP integrations

In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Agents** > **MCP servers** to connect tools such as Linear, GitHub, or Sentry, or add a custom server.

Once configured, click the share icon on a server to make it available to your team.

See the [MCP documentation](/agents/capabilities/mcp/) for full configuration details.

## Troubleshooting

### SSO login issues

For common SSO problems (login failures, account linking, provider portal errors), see [SSO troubleshooting](/enterprise/security-and-compliance/sso/#troubleshooting).

### Team invite links not working

**Common causes:**
* Invite link expired or was revoked
* User's email domain doesn't match configured restrictions

**Solution:**
1. Generate a new invite link.
2. Verify domain restrictions match user's email.
