A workspace groups one or more teams under a single Enterprise account. Workspace admins manage membership, billing, and company-wide policies across those teams.

## Workspaces and teams

A [Warp team](/enterprise/team-management/teams/) groups people and resources so you can manage membership, access, and settings.

Workspaces and teams differ in scope:

* **Workspace level** - A workspace applies billing, SSO, domain capture, and organization-wide policies across its teams.
* **Team level** - A team gives its members access to shared factories, cloud agent runs, environments, secrets, and Warp Drive content within their assigned roles and applicable policies.
* **Membership** - Each user belongs to one workspace and can be a member of multiple teams within that workspace.

To create a team and set its visibility, follow [Creating teams and choosing visibility](/enterprise/team-management/teams/#creating-teams-and-choosing-visibility).

## Workspace roles

* **Member** - Uses Warp within their teams and the policies admins configure.
* **Admin** - Manages membership, [invitations](#inviting-members), billing, [policies](#workspace-settings-and-team-settings), and [spending](#workspace-spending-authority). Administers every team, including teams they don't belong to.
* **Owner** - Has every admin capability and can transfer ownership to another member. Each workspace has one owner.

In team member lists, the **Workspace admin** and **Workspace owner** badges distinguish workspace roles from team roles.

To assign team-level administration, follow [Changing team admins](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/#changing-team-admins).

## Unassigned users

An unassigned user belongs to the workspace but no team. Workspace admins manage their membership and per-user spend limits.

Unassigned users cannot start cloud agent or factory runs until they join a team. Workspace-level billing and policies still apply to them.

## Workspace settings and team settings

Workspace admins configure company-wide policies in the [Admin Panel](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/). For supported settings, they can enforce a value across the workspace or select **Respect Team Setting** to let each team decide.

[Single Sign-On (SSO)](/enterprise/security-and-compliance/sso/) and domain capture apply at the workspace level. Domain capture automatically adds users who sign up with a verified company domain. When a workspace admin enforces a setting, team admins see it as locked.

## Cloud agent run visibility

Workspace admins can view and manage each team-owned {VARS.PLATFORM_RUN} in the workspace, including runs that members of other teams started. Their access matches members of the team that owns the run.

Workspace and team roles don't grant access to runs owned by an individual user. Only the user who owns a run can access it by default, though the owner can choose to [share the session](/platform/viewing-cloud-agent-runs/). Other members can access their own runs and runs owned by their teams.

## Inviting members

Compare [invitation methods](/enterprise/team-management/teams/#inviting-and-joining-members) and review your [Enterprise membership and billing terms](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/) before inviting colleagues.

### Inviting members by email

Workspace admins can invite people to the workspace or a specific team, including a hidden team.

1. Open the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/). Confirm the workspace name.
2. Under "Workspace admin" in the sidebar, click **Members**.
3. Under "Invite by email", click the dropdown beside the email field and select a team or **Workspace only**.
   * A team invitation adds the recipient to that team.
   * **Workspace only** lets the recipient select an open team when accepting. If no open teams are available, the recipient can join the workspace without a team.
4. Enter email addresses separated by commas or spaces, then click **Send**. Confirm the invitations are pending.

If you are a team admin but not a workspace admin:

1. In the Admin Panel sidebar, under "Team admin", click the team name, then **Members**. Confirm the team name.
2. Under "Invite by Email", enter the email addresses, separated by commas or spaces. Click **Send** and confirm the invitations are pending in the team's member list.

### Sharing a workspace invite link

You must be a workspace admin to configure and share the workspace invite link.

1. In the Admin Panel, confirm the workspace. Under "Workspace admin", click **Members**.
2. Toggle on **Workspace invite link**.
3. To limit who can join through the link, enter an approved email domain under "Restrict by domain" and click **Add**. Confirm the domain appears in the list. Repeat for any additional domains before sharing.
4. Click **Copy invite link** and share it with recipients.

To disable the link, turn off **Workspace invite link**. To replace it, click **Reset links**, then **Reset** in the confirmation. The old link no longer works.

### Sharing a team invite link

You must be a team or workspace admin. Team invite links are available only for open teams. Use [email invitations](#inviting-members-by-email) for hidden teams.

1. In the Admin Panel, confirm the workspace. Under "Team admin", click the team name, then **Members**.
2. Confirm the team name. Toggle on **Invite by Link**.
3. Click **Copy invite link** and share it with recipients.

To disable the link, turn off **Invite by Link**. To replace it, click **Reset links**, then **Reset** in the confirmation.

## Joining a workspace or team

### Joining through an invitation

1. Open your invitation link.
2. Sign in to Warp. For an email invitation, use the address that received it. For a domain-restricted workspace link, use an address on an approved domain.
3. Confirm the workspace or team name and the signed-in email address before joining:
   * For a team invitation, click **Join team**.
   * For a workspace invitation, click **Join** beside the open team you want to join. If no open teams are listed, click **Join** beside the workspace name to join without a team.
4. Complete any onboarding prompts.
5. If you joined a team, open **Settings** > **Teams** in the Warp app and confirm the intended team appears. If you joined only the workspace, ask a workspace admin to confirm your workspace membership and add you to a team before starting cloud agent or factory runs.

Ask an admin to replace expired or misaddressed email invitations. If you already belong to another Enterprise workspace, contact its admin before changing membership.

### Joining an open team after signing in

Workspace members without a team can select an open team during web sign-in:

1. Sign in to the [Warp web app](https://app.warp.dev/) with your workspace account.
2. On the team-selection screen, click **Join** beside the intended open team.
3. Complete any onboarding prompts. In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Teams** and confirm the team appears.

Alternatively, find open teams in the Warp app under **Settings** > **Teams**. If no team is available, ask a workspace admin to add you. Hidden teams do not appear in discovery.

## Changing workspace roles

### Promoting or demoting workspace admins

Workspace owners and admins can promote or demote workspace admins. Demotion removes workspace administration, not separately assigned team-admin roles.

1. Open the [Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) and confirm the workspace. Under "Workspace admin" in the sidebar, click **Members**.
2. Next to the person whose role you want to change, click the three dots, then click **Promote to admin** to make them an Admin or **Demote admin** to make them a Member. If you're demoting yourself, click **Demote to member** in the confirmation dialog.
3. Confirm the updated role in the member list.

Admins cannot change the workspace owner's role.

### Transferring workspace ownership

Only the workspace owner can transfer ownership to another member. After the transfer, you become an Admin. Existing team roles do not change.

1. In the Admin Panel, confirm the workspace. Under "Workspace admin", click **Members**.
2. Next to the member who will become the owner, click the three dots, then click **Transfer ownership**.
3. In the "Transfer workspace ownership?" dialog, confirm the recipient and workspace. Click **Transfer ownership**.
4. Confirm the recipient has the **Workspace owner** badge and you have the **Workspace admin** badge.

## Removing a workspace member

Workspace owners and admins can remove workspace members. Removal also removes the person from every team. To remove only one team membership, follow [Removing members from a team](/enterprise/team-management/teams/#removing-members-from-a-team).

Members cannot leave a workspace themselves. Ask an admin to remove you. Owners must transfer ownership before they can be removed.

1. In the Admin Panel, confirm the workspace. Under "Workspace admin", click **Members**.
2. Next to the person you want to remove, click the three dots, then click **Remove from workspace**.
3. Check the email address and workspace in the confirmation. Click **Remove from workspace**.
4. Confirm the person no longer appears in the workspace's member list.
