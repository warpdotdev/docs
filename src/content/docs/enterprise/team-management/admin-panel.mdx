Use the Warp Admin Panel to enforce policies across an Enterprise workspace or delegate supported choices to team admins.

Your [Enterprise role](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/) determines scope. For example, a workspace admin can enforce a policy across Engineering and Support, while an Engineering team admin configures their team within workspace policies and plan restrictions.

<span id="what-is-the-admin-panel" />

## Accessing the Admin Panel

<span id="for-team-admins" />

### For workspace and team admins

1. Open the [Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) and sign in. Confirm the workspace name.
2. To change a workspace policy, open its section under "Workspace admin" in the sidebar.
3. To change a team's settings, click its name under "Team admin", then open the relevant section. Confirm the team name before making a change.

In the {VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}, admins can also open the account menu and click **Admin panel**.

For membership and role changes, use [workspace membership and roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/) or [Changing team admins](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/#changing-team-admins).

<span id="multi-admin-functionality" />
<span id="promoting-and-demoting-admins" />

### For team members

Members see enforced settings in Warp Settings. Ask a team or workspace admin to review a locked control.

## How settings enforcement works

Workspace admins enforce supported policies across teams. Team admins configure their teams within those policies and plan restrictions. Some team controls, such as Models, have no workspace delegation setting.

<span id="toggleable-vs-fixed-settings" />
<span id="setting-inheritance" />
<span id="user-experience" />
<span id="testing-before-enforcement" />

### Setting enforcement levels

| Setting state | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace enforced | Team admins and members cannot override the configured value |
| Respect Team Setting | Each team controls the setting, subject to its plan |
| Respect User Setting | On team controls that offer it, individual members control their preference |
| Tier restricted | The plan does not permit changing the control |

Only controls offering **Respect Team Setting** support delegation.

### Enforcing or delegating workspace policies

You must be a workspace owner or admin. Before enforcing a value, test it with an approved team while the workspace control remains delegated.

To stop content collection for service improvement across the workspace or let teams decide:

1. Open the [Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) and confirm the workspace name.
2. Under "Workspace admin", click **Privacy**.
3. Click the "UGC Data Collection" dropdown. Select **Disabled** to enforce collection off, or **Respect Team Setting** to delegate the choice. A plan-locked control is not editable.
4. Wait for the update to complete. Reload the page and confirm the selected value persists.
5. Under "Team admin", click an affected team's name, then **Privacy**. An enforced value shows "This setting is managed by your workspace." A delegated control remains subject to plan restrictions.

For Terminal team configuration, follow [Admin Panel for Warp Terminal](/knowledge-and-collaboration/admin-panel/). Factory and standalone controls are linked under [Platform settings](#platform-settings).

## Plan limitations

A plan-locked setting shows "Configuring this setting is not available on your plan." Delegation does not remove plan restrictions. Contact your account manager if controls don't match your agreement.

Enterprise adds enforced secret redaction, a control restricting direct API inference to the US, and customer-hosted storage. The inference control is not an all-data residency guarantee.

## AI settings

<span id="general-ai-settings" />

Under "Workspace admin", click **AI** for workspace policies. Open a team's **AI** section under "Team admin" for team controls. These govern supported Warp Agent behavior, not every harness.

### Autonomy settings

Under "Autonomy", workspace approval controls such as "Apply Code Diffs", "Create Plans", "Execute Commands", and "Read Files" offer **Agent Decides**, **Always Ask**, **Always Allow**, and **Respect Team Setting**. A delegated team's control can instead offer **Respect User Setting**.

Click the relevant dropdown and select the required value. Reload and confirm it persists. See [Agent Profiles and permissions](/agents/capabilities/agent-profiles-permissions/) for permission behavior.

### Directory and command control

Under **AI** > "Autonomy", enter a path in "Directory Allowlist" or a regular expression in "Command Allowlist" or "Command Denylist", then press `Enter`. Reload and confirm the entry persists. Team admins see workspace entries as read-only under "Added by your workspace".

:::caution
Autonomy denylist matches require explicit approval. The separate "Sandboxed Agents" denylist blocks matching commands. Autonomy settings do not apply to sandboxed agents.
:::

## Privacy settings

### Content collection for service improvement

Follow [Enforcing or delegating workspace policies](#enforcing-or-delegating-workspace-policies) to change "UGC Data Collection". See [Privacy and data control](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/) for the separate telemetry, provider-retention, and product-storage rules.

<span id="changing-improvement-collection-in-a-browser" />
<span id="changing-improvement-collection-in-the-admin-panel" />
<span id="managing-team-content-collection-in-the-admin-panel" />

For delegated choices, use [Terminal team privacy settings](/knowledge-and-collaboration/admin-panel/#content-collection-for-service-improvement) or [factory data controls](/factories/teams-and-admin-controls/#changing-content-collection). A plan-locked value remains locked.

### Cloud conversation storage

Under "Workspace admin" > **Privacy**, the "Cloud Conversation Storage" dropdown lets you enforce **Enabled** or **Disabled**, or select **Respect Team Setting**. Selection saves immediately. Reload the page and confirm the selected value persists.

This control applies to personal Terminal conversations. Disabling it does not disable factory or other cloud agent run storage. Review [product storage](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/#product-storage) and configure [self-hosted data storage](/factories/self-hosting/data-storage/) separately.

### Enterprise secret redaction

Under "Workspace admin" > **Privacy**, configure "Enterprise Secret Redaction" and its patterns. Follow [Secret redaction](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/secret-redaction/) for configuration and scope. Redaction does not set retention or storage policy.

## Code settings

Under "Workspace admin", click **Code**. Click the "Codebase Indexing" dropdown and select **Disabled**, **Enabled**, or **Respect Team Setting**. Reload the page and confirm the selected value persists.

Repository and file limits depend on the plan. See [Codebase Context](/agents/capabilities/codebase-context/) for indexing behavior and limits.

<span id="models-settings" />

## Model settings

Under "Team admin", click the team name, then **Models**. There is no workspace **Models** section. Available controls include:

* **Enterprise model restrictions** - Enable or disable individual models and configure [BYOLLM routing](/enterprise/enterprise-features/bring-your-own-llm/).
* **Shared provider credentials** - Configure [team-managed API keys and endpoints](/enterprise/enterprise-features/team-managed-keys-and-endpoints/) on plans that include that control. Sharing credentials isn't a model restriction.
* **Enterprise custom routers** - Share [routing rules](/agents/inference/custom-routers/#team-synced-routers) with the team. A router's choices are separate from enforced model availability.

### Models with provider-specific data retention requirements

Some models require provider retention even with content collection disabled. They are exceptions to [Zero Data Retention (ZDR)](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/#zero-data-retention-zdr) and are disabled by default for Enterprise teams. A workspace admin must enable them before use. Review the [model exception list](/agents/inference/model-choice/#models-requiring-provider-retention).

For factory-specific model and retention choices, use [factory ZDR guidance](/factories/zero-data-retention/).

## Billing settings

Under "Workspace admin", click **Billing** for workspace usage and controls. Open a team's **Billing** section under "Team admin" for that team's controls.

Enterprise supports monthly workspace, team, and per-user spend caps, separate local and cloud spending limits, and usage alerts.

See [Enterprise billing](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/) for pools, cap interactions, and contract changes. For factory funding, upgrade, and payment routes, use [Managing factory plans and billing](/factories/managing-plans-and-billing/).

## Sharing settings

Workspace sharing values apply across every team. Team Sharing controls are read-only. Review [Warp Drive sharing](/knowledge-and-collaboration/warp-drive/) before changing access to existing resources.

1. Under "Workspace admin" in the Admin Panel, click **Sharing**. Confirm the workspace name.
2. Click the "Direct Link Sharing" or "Anyone with Link Sharing" dropdown and select **Enabled** or **Disabled**. These controls do not offer **Respect Team Setting**.
3. Wait for the update to complete. Reload and verify both sharing values. Open an affected team's **Sharing** section and confirm its controls are locked.

"Direct Link Sharing" permits direct links to Warp Drive resources. "Anyone with Link Sharing" permits access outside the team. Factory roles and cloud-run visibility are [separate controls](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/#resource-sharing-controls).

## Troubleshooting

### Users can't see new settings

Check the workspace, team, and affected user's membership. If the workspace delegates the setting, check the team's value. Reload the user's Settings before testing again.

### Setting is grayed out in Admin Panel

For "This setting is managed by your workspace", change the workspace policy or delegate it where supported. For plan restrictions, contact your account manager.

### Command allowlist not working

Check workspace-added rules as well as the team's rules. An Autonomy denylist match still requires approval. A sandboxed-agent denylist match blocks execution instead. For personal approval behavior, see [Agent Profiles and permissions](/agents/capabilities/agent-profiles-permissions/).
