A role defines what you can access and which actions you can take in Warp. Your effective permissions also depend on your plan, memberships, and applicable policies.

Enterprise workspace roles apply across teams. Team roles govern administration within one team. Factory roles govern running and configuring factories. For example, an Engineering team admin can administer that team's factories without receiving workspace-wide billing or integration-installation authority.

## Workspace roles and team roles

<span id="user-roles" />
<span id="team-roles" />

| Scope | Role | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace | Owner | Workspace administration plus ownership transfer; exactly one owner |
| Workspace | Admin | Workspace membership, billing, and supported policies; administration of every team, including teams they do not belong to |
| Workspace | Member | Uses Warp through their memberships and applicable policies; no workspace-wide administration |
| Team | Admin | Membership and configurable settings for their team; no automatic workspace authority |
| Team | Member | Team resources within applicable policies and factory access |

Workspace and team roles are additive. Demoting a workspace admin does not remove a separately assigned team-admin role. Workspace membership also does not join every team.

See [Workspace roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#workspace-roles) for workspace definitions and [Changing workspace roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#changing-workspace-roles) for promotion, demotion, and ownership transfer. To end workspace access, follow [Removing a workspace member](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#removing-a-workspace-member).

## Factory roles and team administration

Factory user and Editor are factory-access roles, not team membership roles. Workspace owners and admins inherit Admin access to factories across the workspace. Team admins inherit Admin only for their team's factories. Factory-specific assignments cannot reduce that inherited access.

An Editor can attach an existing shared integration. Establishing a new workspace-owned connection or initiating delegated setup requires a workspace owner or admin. Enterprise team-admin authority alone is not enough. Personal account linking and permissions in the external service are separate.

Follow [Managing factory permissions](/factories/permissions/) for role capabilities, team defaults, and factory-specific assignments, and [Connecting your factory to external tools](/factories/connect-your-factory/) for setup.

## Spending authority

Team admins manage supported spending limits for their team and its members. Workspace owners and admins manage those limits across teams, including unassigned workspace users. See [Enterprise spending controls](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/#spending-controls).

Viewing usage, purchasing usage, changing payment details, and managing subscriptions are separate actions. Do not infer billing-portal access from permission to change a team spending limit.

## Member access and policy limits

<span id="what-members-can-do" />

Members use shared resources and configure personal settings within the applicable policies. A Member role is not a promise of read-only access. A member can separately have factory Editor access.

<span id="settings-enforcement" />

Team admins cannot override workspace-enforced policies. Follow [Enforcing or delegating workspace policies](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/#enforcing-or-delegating-workspace-policies) to change which scope controls a setting.

## Resource sharing controls

Warp Drive sharing policies and cloud-run visibility are separate from role assignments. Use the [Admin Panel sharing settings](/enterprise/team-management/admin-panel/#sharing-settings) and [workspace run-visibility guidance](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#cloud-agent-run-visibility).

Factory roles do not grant repository permissions or permission to merge pull requests.

## Changing team admins

<span id="managing-admins" />
<span id="promoting-or-demoting-admins" />

Workspace admins can assign admins to any team. Team admins can add or remove admins within their own team.

1. Open the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/). Confirm the workspace. Under "Team admin" in the sidebar, click the team name, then **Members**. Confirm the team name.
2. Use the controls shown in the member list:
   * In the member-menu layout, next to the person you want to promote or demote, click the three dots. Click **Promote to team admin** or **Demote team admin**.
   * In the editable "Role" column, click the member's role dropdown. Select **Team Admin** to promote them. To demote an admin, select **Factory Editor** or **Factory User** for the factory access they should retain. This removes team administration and assigns that team-wide factory role.
3. If you're demoting yourself, review the confirmation and click **Demote to member**.
4. Confirm the updated team role. If you lost Admin Panel access, ask another admin to confirm it.

See [factory role capabilities](/factories/permissions/#roles-and-capabilities) before choosing access during demotion. An inherited workspace-admin role cannot be reduced by changing a team or factory role.
