A Warp team groups people and resources so you can manage membership, access, and settings. Team members can share factories, environments, secrets, and Warp Drive content. Teams are available on self-serve and Enterprise plans.

In an Enterprise, a workspace groups one or more teams under a single account. Workspace admins manage membership, billing, and company-wide policies across those teams. Team admins manage their own teams within those policies.

For example, a workspace member can administer the Engineering team without receiving workspace billing authority. See [Enterprise roles and permissions](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/).

## Creating teams and choosing visibility

<span id="creating-a-team" />

You must be a workspace admin to create a team.

1. Open the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/). Confirm the workspace name at the top of the page.
2. Under "Workspace admin" in the sidebar, click **Teams**. Click **New** to open the "Create team" dialog.
3. Enter a team name in the "Name & Color" field.
4. Click the "Team visibility" dropdown and select how colleagues can find and join the team:

   * **Open** - Workspace members can discover the team and join it.
   * **Hidden** - The team does not appear in discovery. An admin adds members directly.

5. Optionally, search for existing workspace members under "Invite members" and select the people to add.
6. Click **Create team**. Confirm the "Team created" message appears and the team is listed on the Teams page.

{/* VERIFY: Exercise this creation procedure in the production Admin Panel. Control labels and success behavior were checked against source; no live-account test was performed. */}

Workspace admins can administer every team, including teams they do not personally belong to. Newly created Enterprise teams do not require a team owner. Ownership belongs to the workspace.

Choose an invitation method based on the access the recipient needs:

| Method | Access | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workspace invitation | Joins the workspace and lets the recipient select an open team | Let colleagues select their team |
| Team invite link | Joins an open team and its workspace | Share access to a specific open team |
| Team email invitation | Joins the named team and its workspace | Invite specific people, including to hidden teams |

Review the [Enterprise membership and billing terms](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/) before inviting colleagues. To send invitations, follow [Inviting members by email](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#inviting-members-by-email), [Sharing a workspace invite link](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#sharing-a-workspace-invite-link), or [Sharing a team invite link](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#sharing-a-team-invite-link).

To accept an invitation, follow [Joining through an invitation](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#joining-through-an-invitation). Workspace membership alone does not join every team. If you belong to the workspace but no team, follow [Joining an open team after signing in](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#joining-an-open-team-after-signing-in).

To add or remove team admins, follow [Changing team admins](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/#changing-team-admins). Workspace admins can manage admins across the workspace. Team admins manage admins for their own team.

To change who can run or configure a factory, follow [Managing factory permissions](/factories/permissions/). Team membership alone does not determine factory configuration access.

## Removing members from a team

<span id="purchased-usage-when-membership-changes" />

<span id="leaving-and-deleting-teams" />
<span id="add-on-credit-consequences-when-leaving-or-removing-members" />

You must be a team or workspace admin to remove a team member. Removing someone from one team keeps their workspace membership and their other team memberships. To remove access across the workspace instead, follow [Removing a workspace member](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#removing-a-workspace-member).

Purchased team usage remains tied to its team when membership changes. Enterprise pools and allocation changes follow the [billing agreement](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/).

1. In the [Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/), confirm the workspace. Under "Team admin" in the sidebar, click the team name.
2. Click **Members**. Confirm the team name at the top of the page.
3. Next to the person you want to remove, click the three dots, then click **Remove from team**.
4. Confirm the email address and removal scope in the dialog. Click **Remove from team** and confirm the person no longer appears in the team's member list.

## Transferring workspace ownership

<span id="transferring-team-admin" />
<span id="transferring-team-ownership" />

Only the workspace owner can transfer ownership. The previous owner becomes an Admin, and existing team roles do not change. Follow [Transferring workspace ownership](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#transferring-workspace-ownership), rather than the self-serve team-ownership procedure.
