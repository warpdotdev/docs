Your Enterprise contract sets usage pools, rates, and billing terms. Contracts that bill in credits continue to do so; dollar amounts in usage reports do not change those terms. Contact your Warp account manager for your team's terms.

<span id="credits" />

## Usage under your contract

### How credits work

Inference usage depends on the model and tokens processed, including prompt caching. Similar prompts can consume different amounts because their context and responses differ.

See [usage and billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/) for charge components and cost reporting.

### Credit allocation

Enterprise plans use a contract-specific shared team pool rather than the per-seat allowances on self-serve plans.

:::note
Credit allocations vary by contract. Contact your account manager for details on your team's specific allocation.
:::

## Cloud agent billing

Cloud runs incur inference, Warp-hosted compute, and [platform charges](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/) according to your contract. Platform usage measures billable active time, not the run's total duration. Self-hosted runs do not incur Warp-hosted compute charges, but platform charges still apply.

### How credits are consumed

Local and cloud usage draw from the shared Enterprise pool. Applicable charges depend on the inference source, hosting, and platform usage.

Agent API key runs use shared team usage. User-created schedules run as their creator by default, or as a cloud agent when configured to do so. Enterprise funding follows your contract.

For more details, see [Access, Billing, and Identity](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/).

## BYOLLM billing

With [BYOLLM](/enterprise/enterprise-features/bring-your-own-llm/), your cloud provider bills inference directly rather than consuming Warp-provided inference usage. Applicable platform and Warp-hosted compute charges remain. External inference costs are not fully represented in Warp's usage totals.

If a BYOLLM request falls back to Warp-provided inference, that request consumes usage at your contract's applicable rate.

## Managing billing

Your Warp account manager handles all billing for your Enterprise contract. Contact your account manager or dedicated Slack/Teams channel for:

* Invoices and payment changes
* Contract modifications
* Credit allocation adjustments

For urgent billing issues, email [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev).

### Spending controls

Administrators can configure monthly spending limits and receive alerts to prevent unexpected charges. Configure these controls in the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) under the **Billing** tab.

#### Monthly spending limits

Enterprise administrators can set monthly spending limits across the following four categories:

* **Cloud spending limit** - Cap monthly spend on cloud agent usage.
* **Local spending limit** - Cap monthly spend on local agent usage in the Warp app.
* **Total spending limit** - Cap combined monthly spend across both cloud and local agents.
* **Per-user spending limit** - Cap monthly spend for any individual user. Set a default that applies to all users, or configure limits on a per-user basis for predictable individual spend.

Spending limits apply to both purchased usage and pay-as-you-go usage.

:::note
These Enterprise limits cap usage, not purchases. Self-serve plans instead offer a [monthly spend limit](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#configuring-a-monthly-spend-limit) for additional purchases and auto-reloads.
:::

To review usage by member, see the [Enterprise Analytics API](/enterprise/enterprise-features/analytics-api/).

Multi-team workspaces can also have a workspace-level cap above their team limits. See [Workspace spend limits](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#workspace-spend-limits).

#### Monthly spend alerts

Warp sends alerts to administrators as team usage approaches each configured spending limit, so you can adjust caps, purchase more credits, or communicate with your team before agent usage is blocked at the cap.

#### Credit pool depletion alerts

For enterprises with credit pools, administrators receive alerts as the team credit pool approaches full consumption. This gives you time to top up your credits or change your reload configuration before agent usage is interrupted for the entire team.
