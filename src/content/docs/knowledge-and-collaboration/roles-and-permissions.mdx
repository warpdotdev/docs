A role defines what you can access and which actions you can take in Warp. Your effective permissions also depend on your plan, memberships, and applicable policies.

In {VARS.WARP_TERMINAL}, team roles govern membership and shared settings. For example, a team admin can manage invitations, while a member uses shared Warp Drive resources within the configured sharing policies.

## Checking your role

1. In {VARS.WARP_TERMINAL}, open **Settings** > **Teams**.
2. Confirm the team name and find your email address in the member list.
3. For a self-serve team, read the badge beside your email address. "OWNER" identifies the team owner, and "ADMIN" identifies a team admin. Members have neither badge.

If the member list doesn't identify your role or you need help, ask the person who invited you or a known admin. If you cannot identify an admin, [contact support](mailto:support@warp.dev).

## Team roles

<span id="user-roles" />

Team roles determine who can manage membership, change shared settings, and transfer ownership:

| Role | Responsibilities | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Team owner | Administers a self-serve team and can transfer ownership to another member | Exactly one owner per self-serve team; plan restrictions still apply |
| Team admin | Manages the team's membership, invitations, and configurable settings; can add admins on supported plans | Cannot transfer ownership or change the owner's role; does not automatically gain workspace administration |
| Member | Uses team resources within the applicable policies | Membership alone does not grant team administration |

Adding an admin does not add or replace the owner.

<span id="workspace-roles-and-team-roles" />

Workspace roles apply to Enterprise administration. Self-serve customers manage team roles instead. Enterprise ownership belongs to the workspace, so teams inside it do not require a team owner. See [Workspace roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#workspace-roles) for ownership and [Enterprise roles and permissions](/enterprise/team-management/roles-and-permissions/) for authority across teams.

### Promoting or demoting admins

You must be a team owner or admin to promote or demote team admins. Your plan must support additional admins before you can promote a member.

1. Open the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) and click **Teams**. Confirm the team name at the top of the page.
2. Use the controls shown in the member list:
   * In the member-menu layout, next to the person you want to promote or demote, click the three dots. Click **Promote to team admin** or **Demote team admin**.
   * In the editable "Role" column, click the member's role dropdown. Select **Team Admin** to promote them. To demote an admin, select **Factory Editor** or **Factory User** for the factory access they should retain. This removes team administration and assigns that team-wide factory role.
3. If you're demoting yourself, review the confirmation and click **Demote to member**.
4. Confirm the updated role in the member list. If you lost Admin Panel access, ask another admin to confirm it.

For the difference between the factory roles offered during demotion, see [Managing factory permissions](/factories/permissions/#roles-and-capabilities).

## Permission details

<span id="factory-roles-and-team-administration" />

Factory access is separate from team administration in {VARS.WARP_TERMINAL}. To manage who can run or configure a factory, follow [Managing factory permissions](/factories/permissions/). To manage factory-team membership and admins, follow [Warp Factories team administration](/factories/teams-and-admin-controls/).

### What admins can do

The controls available to admins depend on the plan and any enforced workspace policies.

Where the plan includes spending controls, team admins set limits for their team and its members. Enterprise workspace owners and admins manage those limits across teams. See [Enterprise spending controls](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/#spending-controls) for usage caps and [self-serve purchase limits](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#configuring-a-monthly-spend-limit) for limits on additional purchases.

Viewing usage, purchasing usage, changing payment details, and managing subscriptions are separate actions. See [usage visibility](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/#who-can-see-team-usage) for reporting access and [managing billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/pricing-faqs/#how-do-i-manage-my-billing) for payment and subscription controls.

### What members can do

Members can configure personal settings where team and workspace policies permit. A Member role is not a promise of read-only access to shared resources.

### Settings enforcement

A team-admin role does not override a workspace policy or make a plan-locked setting editable. See [How settings enforcement works](/knowledge-and-collaboration/admin-panel/#how-settings-enforcement-works) for the difference.

## Transferring team ownership

This procedure applies to a self-serve team. Only the current owner can transfer ownership.

After the transfer, you become a Member and lose admin access. If the new owner's email is not on a work domain, Warp disables team discoverability.

1. Open the [Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/), confirm the team, and click **Teams**.
2. Next to the member who will become the owner, click the three dots, then click **Transfer ownership**. If the list uses an editable role column instead, click that member's role dropdown and select **Owner**.
3. In the confirmation, click **Transfer**. The page reloads with your updated access.
4. Ask the new owner to confirm their Owner role.

If you still need admin access, ask the new owner or another admin to grant it separately, if your plan supports additional admins.

In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Teams**. Next to the member who will become the owner, click the three dots, then click **Transfer ownership**.

For Enterprise ownership, follow [Transferring workspace ownership](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#transferring-workspace-ownership). Transferring workspace ownership does not transfer team roles.

## Resource sharing controls

Team roles are distinct from the controls on shared resources. For Warp Drive sharing policies, see the [Admin Panel](/knowledge-and-collaboration/admin-panel/#sharing-settings).

## Troubleshooting

### The admin promotion option is missing

Confirm the selected team and compare its plan with the [admin availability table](#managing-admins). The promotion option is not shown on Free accounts. Purchasing usage does not enable additional admins. Review [plans](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/) before changing a subscription.

In an Enterprise workspace, check whether you are changing a team role or a workspace role. Ask a workspace admin to handle workspace-wide changes.

### Factory settings work, but administration access is denied

Follow [Warp Factories team administration](/factories/teams-and-admin-controls/#checking-your-role) to identify your role and the admin who can help. Factory Editor access does not grant team or workspace administration.
