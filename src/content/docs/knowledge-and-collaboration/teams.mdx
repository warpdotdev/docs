A Warp team is a group of people who share resources, including factories, cloud agent environments, secrets, and [Warp Drive resources](/knowledge-and-collaboration/warp-drive/). Teams are available on self-serve plans as well as Enterprise.

For example, teammates can submit work to a shared factory instead of each creating their own. Membership, administrative roles, and factory access are separate controls. See [Roles and permissions](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/).

<span id="what-is-a-team" />

## Teams and workspaces

On self-serve plans, your team is the group you manage. It has one owner and, on supported plans, additional team admins. Some billing or shared-setting screens use "workspace" for that team's administrative scope; you do not manage a separate set of workspace roles.

Enterprise workspaces can contain multiple teams. Workspace admins manage company-wide settings and team organization, while team admins manage their own teams within those policies. An Enterprise workspace with one team can still use this independent administration model.

See [Workspaces for Enterprise teams](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/) for workspace membership, roles, and team visibility.

## Creating a team

If your self-serve account has no team, initial setup in the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a> can create your first team:

1. Sign in. If setup lists discoverable teams, choose **Create a new team** and enter a team name when requested.
2. Click **Build your first Factory**. If setup does not show a team-name field, Warp creates a default-named team. After completing setup, confirm the selected team in the account menu.

If setup says "Contact an admin to join a team," ask your admin for an invitation. The factory account menu's **New Team** control is for Enterprise workspace administration, not a general self-serve team-creation route.

To create a self-serve team in the Warp app:

1. Open **Settings** > **Teams**, or open **Warp Drive** and click **+ Create a team**.
2. Enter a name that colleagues will recognize and complete the creation flow.
3. Confirm the new team appears in **Settings** > **Teams**.

The creator becomes the team owner. Adding an admin later does not transfer ownership.

In an Enterprise workspace, a workspace admin creates teams from the Admin Panel. See [Teams inside a workspace](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#teams-inside-a-workspace).

To rename a self-serve team, open **Settings** > **Teams** in the Warp app. Click the team name, enter the new name, and press `Enter`.

## Inviting team members

Check the billing consequence for your product before sharing an invitation:

* **Warp Terminal and local agents** - Paid per-seat subscriptions include all active members. Adding a member adds a prorated seat charge. See [member billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/pricing-faqs/#what-counts-as-a-team-member-and-how-does-billing-work-for-members).
* **Warp Factories** - Start uses purchased usage without a subscription; Scale uses one team subscription rather than per-member subscription fees. Members' factory runs consume shared funding, so additional activity can increase usage charges. See [factory plans and funding](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#warp-factories-plans).
* **Enterprise** - Membership charges and usage funding follow your [contract](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/).

Self-serve team owners and admins can invite colleagues in a browser:

1. Open the [Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/), confirm the intended team, and click **Teams**.
2. Under **Invite by Email**, enter the addresses and click **Send**. Confirm they appear as pending invitations.
3. To share a link instead, enable **Invite by Link** if needed. Click the copy control beside the link and share it through a secure channel.

Non-admins can ask an owner or admin for an invite link. In the Warp app, the link is also available under **Settings** > **Teams**.

For Enterprise invitations, including adding people to Hidden teams, follow [Joining a workspace or team](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#joining-a-workspace-or-team).

### Restricting team invites by domain

Team owners and admins can restrict joining through self-serve invite links to approved email domains:

1. In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Teams**.
2. Toggle **Restrict by domain** on.
3. Add the allowed email domains.

In the browser Admin Panel, open **Teams** and enable **Invite by Link**. Under "Restrict by domain," enter an approved domain and click **Add**. Confirm the domain appears in the list.

Someone joining through a link with an address outside the allowed domains must verify access to an address on an allowed domain.

## Joining a team

Open the invitation link in a browser and sign up or sign in with the invited account. Check the team name and click **Join team**. For restricted-domain links, complete the requested email verification first.

Return to the {VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}, reload if needed, and confirm the joined team appears in the account menu. Email invitations must match the invited email address and be unexpired; ask an admin for a new invitation if needed.

Workspace membership does not automatically make you a member of every Enterprise team. For team discovery and workspace invitations, see [Workspaces for Enterprise teams](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/).

## Team discoverability

For eligible self-serve teams, an owner or admin can let colleagues with the same email domain find and join the team without a direct invitation.

In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Teams** and toggle **Make team discoverable** on. In the browser Admin Panel, open **Teams** and enable **Team Discovery** when that control is available. People who join this way follow the same [product-specific billing rules](#inviting-team-members) as invited members.

## Team roles and permissions

Team owners and admins manage membership and the settings available on their plan. Members use shared resources within the applicable policies.

See [Roles and permissions](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/) for role definitions, multiple-admin availability, and changing a role. To configure shared settings, open the [Admin Panel](/knowledge-and-collaboration/admin-panel/).

### Transferring team ownership

<span id="transferring-team-admin" />

Only the current self-serve team owner can transfer ownership to another member. Follow [Transferring team ownership](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/#transferring-team-ownership). The previous owner becomes a Member.

Independent Enterprise workspace ownership is a different operation. Follow [Changing workspace roles](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#changing-workspace-roles).

## Leaving and deleting teams

In a self-serve team, members and admins can leave from **Settings** > **Teams** in the Warp app. An owner must transfer ownership before leaving or [deleting their Warp account](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/#delete-your-account-and-data).

To delete a self-serve team, the owner must first remove the other members.

:::caution
A team cannot be deleted while it has unused purchased usage. Contact [support@warp.dev](mailto:support@warp.dev) for help removing that balance.
:::

Enterprise workspace membership is managed separately from team membership. Ask a workspace admin to remove you from an Enterprise workspace.

<span id="add-on-credit-consequences-when-leaving-or-removing-members" />

### Purchased usage when membership changes

Purchased usage stays tied to the team that bought it. Leaving or being removed ends your access to that balance; rejoining restores access to unused, unexpired usage. Rejoining a per-seat subscription also adds a prorated seat charge.

See [usage when team membership changes](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#when-team-membership-changes). Enterprise pools follow your [billing agreement](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/).

## Managing a team without Warp Terminal

In the {VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}, owners and admins open **Admin panel** from the account menu. In the Admin Panel, choose **Teams** for the membership procedures above or **Billing** for shared funding.

For your access level and an admin handoff, see [Checking your role](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/#checking-your-role). For role changes, follow [promoting admins](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/#promoting-or-demoting-admins) or [transferring ownership](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/#transferring-team-ownership).
