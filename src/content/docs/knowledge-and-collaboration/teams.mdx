A Warp team groups people and resources so you can manage membership, access, and settings. Team members can share factories, environments, secrets, and [Warp Drive content](/knowledge-and-collaboration/warp-drive/). Teams are available on self-serve and Enterprise plans.

In {VARS.WARP_TERMINAL}, teammates can maintain a shared Warp Drive runbook instead of each saving their own copy. Team roles govern membership and administration. Sharing controls govern access to the content. See [Roles and permissions in Warp Terminal](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/).

<span id="what-is-a-team" />

## Teams and workspaces

On self-serve plans, your team is the group you manage. It has one owner and, on supported plans, additional team admins. Some billing or shared-setting screens use "workspace" for that team's administrative scope. You do not manage a separate set of workspace roles.

An Enterprise workspace groups one or more teams under a single Enterprise account. Workspace admins manage membership, billing, and company-wide policies across those teams. Team admins manage their own teams within those policies.

See [Workspaces for Enterprise teams](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/) for workspace membership, roles, and team visibility.

## Creating a team

To create a self-serve team in {VARS.WARP_TERMINAL}:

1. Open **Settings** > **Teams**, or open **Warp Drive** and click **+ Create a team**.
2. In the "Team name" field, enter a name that colleagues will recognize, then click **Create**.
3. Confirm the new team appears in **Settings** > **Teams**.

The creator becomes the team owner. Adding an admin later does not transfer ownership.

In an Enterprise workspace, a workspace admin creates teams from the Admin Panel. Follow [Creating teams and choosing visibility](/enterprise/team-management/teams/#creating-teams-and-choosing-visibility).

### Renaming a team

You must be a team owner or admin to rename a self-serve team.

1. In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Teams** and confirm the intended team.
2. Click the team name, enter the new name, and press `Enter`.
3. Confirm the updated name appears at the top of the page.

## Inviting team members

Before sharing an invitation, check the billing consequence. Paid per-seat plans include all active members. Adding a member adds a prorated seat charge. See [member billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/pricing-faqs/#what-counts-as-a-team-member-and-how-does-billing-work-for-members). Enterprise membership charges follow your [contract](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/).

The following Admin Panel procedures require owner or admin access to a self-serve team.

### Inviting by email

1. Open the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) and click **Teams**. Confirm the team name before inviting colleagues.
2. Under "Invite by Email", enter email addresses separated by commas or spaces, then click **Send**. Confirm the invitations are pending.

### Inviting by link

1. In the Admin Panel, open **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Toggle on **Invite by Link**.
3. To limit joining to approved email domains, enter a domain under "Restrict by domain", then click **Add**. Confirm it appears in the list. Repeat for any additional domains before sharing.
4. Click **Copy invite link** and share the link through a secure channel.

Non-admins can ask an owner or admin for an invite link. In the Warp app, the link is also available under **Settings** > **Teams**.

For Enterprise invitations, including adding people to hidden teams, follow [Joining a workspace or team](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#joining-a-workspace-or-team).

### Restricting team invites by domain

To configure the same restrictions in {VARS.WARP_TERMINAL}, you must be a team owner or admin:

1. Open **Settings** > **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Under "Invite team members", toggle on invite links in the "By link" section.
3. Enter the approved domains in the "Domains, comma separated" field, then click **Set**.
4. Confirm the domains appear in the approved-domain list.

Someone joining through a link with an address outside the allowed domains must verify access to an address on an allowed domain.

## Joining a team

For an email invitation, use the address that received it. Ask an admin to replace an expired or misaddressed invitation.

1. Open the invitation link and sign up or sign in to Warp.
2. Confirm the team name and signed-in email address. For a domain-restricted link, complete any requested email verification.
3. Click **Join team**.
4. Return to {VARS.WARP_TERMINAL}. In **Settings** > **Teams**, confirm the joined team appears.

Workspace membership does not automatically join every Enterprise team. For Enterprise invitations and discovery, follow [Joining a workspace or team](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#joining-a-workspace-or-team).

## Team discoverability

For eligible self-serve teams, an owner or admin can let colleagues with the same email domain find and join the team without a direct invitation.

People who join through discovery follow the same [member billing rules](#inviting-team-members) as invited members.

To enable discovery in {VARS.WARP_TERMINAL}:

1. Open **Settings** > **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Under "Invite team members", toggle on **By discovery**.
3. Reopen **Settings** > **Teams** and confirm discovery remains on.

To enable discovery in the Admin Panel:

1. Open **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Toggle on **Team Discovery** when the control is available.
3. Reload the page and confirm discovery remains on.

## Team roles and permissions

Team owners and admins manage membership and the settings available on their plan. Members use shared resources within the applicable policies.

See [Roles and permissions](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/) for role definitions, multiple-admin availability, and changing a role. To configure shared settings, open the [Admin Panel](/knowledge-and-collaboration/admin-panel/).

### Transferring team ownership

<span id="transferring-team-admin" />

Only the current self-serve team owner can transfer ownership to another member. Follow [Transferring team ownership](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/#transferring-team-ownership). The previous owner becomes a Member.

Enterprise workspace ownership is a different operation. Follow [Transferring workspace ownership](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#transferring-workspace-ownership).

## Leaving and deleting teams

These procedures apply to self-serve teams. Enterprise workspace membership is managed separately. To leave an Enterprise workspace, ask a workspace admin to [remove your membership](/enterprise/team-management/workspaces/#removing-a-workspace-member).

<span id="add-on-credit-consequences-when-leaving-or-removing-members" />

### Purchased usage when membership changes

Purchased usage stays tied to the team that bought it. Leaving or being removed ends your access to that balance. Rejoining restores access to unused, unexpired usage. Rejoining a per-seat subscription also adds a prorated seat charge.

See [usage when team membership changes](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#when-team-membership-changes). Enterprise pools follow your [billing agreement](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/).

### Leaving a team

Members and admins can leave a self-serve team. If you own the team, [transfer ownership](/knowledge-and-collaboration/roles-and-permissions/#transferring-team-ownership) before leaving or [deleting your Warp account](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/#delete-your-account-and-data).

1. In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Click **Leave team**.
3. Review the access changes in the confirmation. Click **Yes, leave** or **Leave Team**, depending on the confirmation shown.
4. Confirm the team is no longer shown as your active team in Settings.

### Deleting a team

Only the owner can delete a self-serve team. Remove the other members first. An active paid subscription or unused purchased usage blocks deletion. If **Delete team** is disabled, review the requirement shown beside it. Use [managing billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/pricing-faqs/#how-do-i-manage-my-billing) for subscription changes or [contact support](mailto:support@warp.dev) for help with unused usage.

Deletion is permanent and removes the team's related content. Review what the team contains before proceeding.

1. In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Teams** and confirm the team name.
2. Click **Delete team**.
3. Review the deletion warning and click **Yes, delete**.
4. Confirm the team no longer appears in Settings.
