Warp lets you connect your **ChatGPT plan** so OpenAI models run through your own OpenAI account. If you already pay for ChatGPT, you can use OpenAI models in Warp without spending your Warp [credits](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/). OpenAI determines which plan tiers are eligible and how much usage they include.

Connecting a plan works like [Bring Your Own API Key (BYOK)](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/), except you sign in to your OpenAI account instead of pasting an API key. It covers OpenAI models only — Anthropic, Google, and xAI models are unaffected.

:::note
Like BYOK, connecting a ChatGPT plan requires that your team allows members to bring their own keys. See [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing) for current availability.
:::

## How it works

When you connect your plan, Warp opens your browser to OpenAI's Sign in with ChatGPT consent screen. Approving it links your ChatGPT account to your Warp account and authorizes OpenAI to share a delegated credential with Warp. Unlike a BYOK API key or a [SuperGrok subscription](/agents/inference/grok-subscription/), that credential is held for your account by Warp's backend rather than in your device's keychain, so you connect once per Warp account rather than once per device.

With a plan connected, Warp attaches the delegated credential to OpenAI requests the Warp Agent makes on your behalf, and OpenAI bills them to your ChatGPT plan. A pasted OpenAI API key takes precedence: if you have both, the key wins and the plan goes unused.

Connecting also changes which models you see. The server recomputes your model catalog when the connection starts or stops, and OpenAI models that your Warp plan would otherwise gate behind an upgrade become selectable, since you are paying OpenAI for them directly.

### What OpenAI sees

Each subscription-billed request carries two fixed labels that OpenAI requires: an activity name and a short purpose string, such as "Generate the agent's next response in a Warp agent conversation." The labels are fixed per request type and never derived from your prompt. OpenAI stores them separately from the request content and can surface them in your ChatGPT usage settings.

Agent turns are not the only requests that carry them. Background work the agent does for you — generating a conversation title, compacting earlier history to free context-window space, condensing a large tool result — is also billed to your plan and labeled the same way.

:::caution
Data retention on OpenAI's side is governed by your own OpenAI account and its terms. Warp's Zero Data Retention agreements cover Warp-managed inference, so they do not apply to requests billed to your plan. See [Zero Data Retention and BYOK](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/#zero-data-retention-zdr-and-byok).
:::

## Connecting your ChatGPT plan

1. In the Warp app, go to **Settings** > **Agents** > **Warp Agent** and find the "Use OpenAI ChatGPT plan" row under **Custom Inference**.
2. Click **Continue with ChatGPT**. Warp opens your browser and shows a toast confirming the attempt. Click **Cancel** in Warp if you want to abandon it.
3. Approve the connection in your browser. Back in Warp, the row shows a green check with "Connected as YOUR_EMAIL on DATE," where YOUR_EMAIL is the address on your ChatGPT account.
4. Select an OpenAI model from the model picker in your prompt input. Connecting a plan doesn't change your selected model, so this step is what actually routes requests through your plan.

The first time a connected plan funds a request, Warp shows a one-time notice titled "You're using your ChatGPT plan" with a **Manage usage** button that opens your [ChatGPT usage settings](https://chatgpt.com/settings/usage). OpenAI's Sign in with ChatGPT guidelines require it.

If the row shows "Reconnect to use your plan for requests" next to the connected timestamp, the link still exists but OpenAI is no longer sharing a credential. Disconnect and connect again.

Connecting is only available in the Warp app. The [Warp Agent CLI](/agents/cli/) has no equivalent command, unlike `/connect-grok` for a SuperGrok subscription.

## Usage and billing behavior

When an OpenAI model runs on your connected plan:

* Warp **does not consume** any of your [credits](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/) for the request.
* Inference is billed to your ChatGPT plan, within OpenAI's usage limits for that plan.
* Review and manage that usage in your [ChatGPT usage settings](https://chatgpt.com/settings/usage), not in Warp. The **Billing and Usage** settings page links out to the same place.

### When OpenAI refuses a request

OpenAI can reject a subscription-billed request — for example, when your plan runs out of shared usage. Warp surfaces OpenAI's own message along with the recovery options OpenAI offers, which can include:

* **Retry** - Send the turn again, still billed to your plan.
* **Continue with Warp credits** - Switch the rest of this conversation to Warp-funded inference and resume. This applies to that conversation only; new conversations go back to using your plan.
* A link to an OpenAI page, such as your usage settings.

Choosing **Continue with Warp credits** is one-way for that conversation. Later failures in it are shown as a short disclosure line rather than an error, because the conversation is no longer using your plan.

### Auto models

Warp's **Auto** models always use Warp's own inference and **always consume Warp credits**, even with a ChatGPT plan connected. To use your plan, select a specific OpenAI model from the model picker.

## Disconnecting

In the Warp app, go to **Settings** > **Agents** > **Warp Agent**, find the "Use OpenAI ChatGPT plan" row, and click **Disconnect**. Warp unlinks the account on its servers, and OpenAI models go back to running on Warp credits. Connecting again re-shows the one-time plan notice.

## Troubleshooting

### "That ChatGPT account is already connected to another Warp account"

Each ChatGPT account links to one Warp account, and each Warp account links to one ChatGPT account. Disconnect the existing link first, then connect again.

### "Verify the email on your ChatGPT account, then try again"

OpenAI refuses to share a plan from an account with an unverified email. Verify it with OpenAI and retry.

### "Your browser is signed in to a different ChatGPT account"

The browser completed the flow as an account other than the one you intended. Sign out of ChatGPT in your browser, or use a private window, then click **Continue with ChatGPT** again.

### "Warp needs your permission in the browser"

The consent screen was dismissed or denied. Click **Continue with ChatGPT** and approve it.
