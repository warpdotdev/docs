## General

### What data is sent and/or stored when using Agents in Warp?

See our [Privacy page](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/) for more information on how we handle data used by Agents in Warp.

### What happened to the old Warp AI chat panel?

Agent Mode has replaced the previous AI chat panel. Agent Mode is more powerful in all of the chat panel's use cases. Not only can Agent Mode run commands for you, it can also gather context without you needing to copy and paste. To start a similar chat panel, click the AI button in the menu bar to open a new AI pane.

### Is my data used for model training?

Warp reserves the right to use data collected to train models and improve Warp. Warp has Zero Data Retention with all its model providers (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI, etc.). Learn more about telemetry on our [Privacy page](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/).

### What model are you using for Agent Mode?

Warp supports a curated list of LLMs from providers like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Gemini. To view the full list of supported models and learn how to switch between them, visit the [Model Choice](/agents/inference/model-choice/) page.

### Can I use my own LLM API key?

Yes. For Warp Terminal and local agents, [personal BYOK](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/) connects your Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google key to your provider account. It is available on the Free plan and eligible paid plans for individuals and organizations with 10 or fewer employees, subject to Warp's Terms of Service. Larger organizations need a Business or Enterprise plan for Warp Terminal and local agents.

For Warp Factories, the Scale and Enterprise plans support [team-managed keys and endpoints](/enterprise/enterprise-features/team-managed-keys-and-endpoints/). An admin configures shared credentials for factory inference; a key stored on a member's device is not available to factory runs. An Enterprise agreement can also include [BYOLLM](/enterprise/enterprise-features/bring-your-own-llm/) cloud-provider routing.

### Can I use a ChatGPT or Claude subscription with Warp?

You can [connect a ChatGPT subscription](/agents/inference/chatgpt-subscription/) to use eligible OpenAI models with the Warp Agent in the Warp app. You can connect while signing up or signing in, or link an existing Warp account from Settings.

Warp doesn't support connecting a Claude consumer subscription. To use your own Anthropic account, add an API key with [Bring Your Own API Key (BYOK)](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/).

For xAI's Grok models, connect a [SuperGrok subscription](/agents/inference/grok-subscription/).

## Billing

### Warp Terminal and local agents

Build, Max, and Business subscriptions for Warp Terminal and local agents have per-seat allowances. On plans billed in dollars, Warp-provided inference is priced at public API rates.

On the Free plan for Warp Terminal and local agents, you can [purchase usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#warp-terminal-and-local-agents) without subscribing. You can also [bring your own inference](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/).

### Warp Factories

The Scale plan for Warp Factories has a shared monthly team allowance. The Start plan for Warp Factories uses purchased usage without a subscription. Factory runs do not use a member's included personal allowance. See [factory plans and funding](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#warp-factories-plans) and [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing).

See [usage and billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/) for metered features and token details, and [plans, pricing, and refunds](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/) for resets and existing balances.

## Common AI error messages

#### "Message token limit exceeded" error

This error means your input (plus attached context) exceeds the maximum context window of the model you're using. If you exceed the limit for your selected model, you may receive no output.

To fix this, try:

* Starting a new conversation
* Reducing the number of blocks or lines attached to your query

#### "Monthly request limit exceeded" or "Monthly credit limit exceeded" errors

These errors mean you've exhausted your available usage balance, so Warp-provided inference pauses. In the Warp app, check **Settings** > **Billing and usage** for your balance and reset date.

To continue, [purchase more usage or enable auto-reload](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/), [bring your own inference](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/), or wait for your next included-usage reset if your plan has an allowance. Enterprise [billing and spending controls](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/) follow your contract.

**Request failed with error: QuotaLimit**

A usage quota has been reached. In the Warp app, check **Settings** > **Billing and usage** for your available balance. For help identifying the limit, [contact support](/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/sending-us-feedback/).

**Request failed with error: ErrorStatus (403, "Your account has been blocked from using AI features")**

This message means your account has been blocked from using AI features, typically due to a violation of our [Terms of Service](https://www.warp.dev/legal/terms-of-service) or suspected abuse (e.g. attempting to bypass credit or token limits).

To resolve or clarify this, please contact our team at [appeals@warp.dev](mailto:appeals@warp.dev) if you believe this was an error. We'll review your case and respond as soon as possible.

:::caution
Note that any error that does not mention appeals@warp.dev isn't related to being blocked and should be reported as feedback or a bug. See [Sending Us Feedback](/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/sending-us-feedback/) for more.
:::

## Gathering AI conversation ID

In cases where you have issues with the Agent, we may ask for the AI conversation ID to troubleshoot the specific conversation. To gather the conversation ID, see [Gathering AI conversation ID](/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/sending-us-feedback/#gathering-ai-conversation-id) for detailed steps.
