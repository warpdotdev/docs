## Warp Factories

### Which plan should my team choose for Warp Factories?

Choose the Start plan for usage-based factory work without a subscription, the Scale plan for shared monthly usage and team-managed inference, or an Enterprise plan for custom terms and self-hosted execution. These are Warp Factories plans, not Warp Terminal subscriptions. See [factory plan guidance](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#warp-factories-plans).

### Does my Warp Terminal subscription fund factory runs?

Your included personal allowance does not fund factory runs. Factories use shared team or workspace funding, including the shared allowance on the Scale plan for Warp Factories and eligible purchased usage. See [funding factory runs](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#funding-factory-runs).

### Where do I manage billing for Warp Factories?

In the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a>, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage**. Admins can click **Open billing** to review the applicable team or workspace in the Admin Panel. Follow [managing factory plans and billing](/factories/managing-plans-and-billing/) for upgrades, invoices, and support.

## Warp Terminal

### How can I upgrade and subscribe to a Warp plan?

You can subscribe to a paid Warp Terminal plan as an individual or as part of a team. To upgrade:

* In the app: Go to **Settings** > **Billing and usage**, then click the Upgrade link
* Online: See [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing)

After entering your payment details, you’ll receive an invoice and confirmation via email.

### How can I get the most out of my Warp plan?

Choose a Warp Terminal plan based on usage and team controls:

* **Build** - A monthly included allowance for individual development or a small team's agent work, with additional usage purchases when needed.
* **Max** - A larger included allowance for heavier agent usage.
* **Business** - Per-seat billing, admin-configurable data controls, and SAML SSO.
* **Enterprise** - Custom allowances and terms, managed [BYOLLM](/enterprise/enterprise-features/bring-your-own-llm/), and contract-specific administration and support.

Check **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the Warp app for your account's allowance and reset date. For automated workflows, compare the separate [Warp Factories plans](#which-plan-should-my-team-choose-for-warp-factories). See [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing) for current prices and limits.

### How can I subscribe to a Warp Enterprise plan?

For Warp Terminal, the self-serve Business plan supports teams that need SAML SSO and admin-configurable data controls. Choose an Enterprise plan when your organization needs custom pricing, shared usage pools, managed cloud-provider inference, or contract-specific support. Existing Enterprise contracts retain their terms. See [Enterprise billing](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/).

For Warp Factories, the Scale plan is the self-serve team subscription; an Enterprise plan adds custom usage terms and self-hosted execution. See [factory plans and funding](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#warp-factories-plans).

:::tip
If you’d like to explore Enterprise, [contact our sales team](https://www.warp.dev/contact-sales) and someone from Warp will follow up.
:::

Provider ZDR and analytics opt-out are separate from your plan's model controls. See [Warp's provider ZDR policy](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/#zero-data-retention-zdr) and [factory ZDR guidance](/factories/zero-data-retention/) for coverage, exceptions, and what self-serve teams can configure.

### What counts as a team member and how does billing work for members?

For Warp Terminal subscriptions, a _team member_ is any seat with access to your team, including shared Warp Drive resources. Paid subscriptions apply to the team, including your own account and all active members. See [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing) for current plan limits.

On the Free plan, you can share a limited number of Notebooks and Workflows with other Free users. If your team exceeds that limit, you’ll be prompted to [upgrade to a plan](https://app.warp.dev/upgrade).\
\
On Build, Max, and Business subscriptions for Warp Terminal, billing is prorated: you're charged for the days each member has access during the billing cycle. If a member leaves mid-cycle, Warp applies a credit for unused time to your next invoice.

For those per-seat subscriptions, the payment schedule determines when new members are billed:

* **Monthly plans**: New members are billed immediately for the remaining time in the month.
* **Annual plans**: New members are billed immediately for the remaining time in the year.

For example, a member joining halfway through a monthly billing cycle incurs half of that seat's monthly fee. A member joining with four months remaining in an annual cycle is charged for those four months. Upgrading the team includes all active members, not only the admin.

:::caution
Within a team's Terminal and agent subscription, all members have the same plan. You can't mix per-seat plans within that subscription. This is separate from choosing a Warp Factories plan.
:::

### What is the value of joining or creating a team on Warp?

Teams share [Warp Drive](/knowledge-and-collaboration/warp-drive/) resources and collaborate through features such as [Session Sharing](/agents/local-agents/session-sharing/). Access and limits depend on the plan; see [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing). For automated team workflows, see [Warp Factories](/factories/).

### My co-workers are using Warp but we’re not on a Team together yet. How does billing work?

Sharing an email domain does not create a paid team subscription. You can use Warp independently under your account's plan, or [create a team](/knowledge-and-collaboration/teams/) to share Warp Drive resources.

Before inviting colleagues, review [member billing by product](/knowledge-and-collaboration/teams/#inviting-team-members). Per-seat subscriptions and Warp Factories team subscriptions bill membership differently.

### How does usage work if logged into the same account on multiple devices?

When you sign into Warp on different devices, your metered usage is tracked at the account level, not separately for each device.

You can use your Warp account on multiple personal computers. When you log in with the same account on different computers, your settings and preferences can be synced through the [Settings Sync](/terminal/more-features/settings-sync/) feature.

## Shared usage and billing

### How do I manage my billing?

For a self-serve team, the owner or an admin opens the billing portal to update payment details, view invoices, or manage the subscription. Non-admin members cannot open the portal; ask an admin to make those changes. Permission to [purchase eligible shared usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#who-can-purchase-usage) is separate.

Enterprise customers contact their Warp account manager for payment and contract changes instead.

You can open **Manage billing** from the Warp app or the web:

* **In the Warp app** - Go to **Settings** > **Billing and usage**, then click **Manage billing**.
* **On the web** - Open the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) or the [Warp upgrade page](https://app.warp.dev/upgrade), click your profile in the top-right corner, then click **Manage billing**. You don't need the Warp app installed.

For Warp Factories, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the {VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}, then click **Open billing**.

### What happens when I downgrade during a billing cycle?

Downgrades take effect immediately. Warp issues an account balance for the prorated difference based on the time remaining in your billing cycle, not on unused included usage. You can apply that balance toward future subscription payments or usage purchases. The account balance amount is visible in the billing portal invoice history.

You can downgrade at any point throughout your subscription through the billing portal. See [How do I manage my billing?](#how-do-i-manage-my-billing).

### How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel at any point throughout your subscription through the billing portal. See [How do I manage my billing?](#how-do-i-manage-my-billing).

Cancelled subscriptions remain active until the end of your monthly or annual billing cycle. You keep paid-plan features until that date; cancellation does not immediately refund the unused portion. See [refund policies](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#warps-refund-policies) if you need a refund.

For per-seat Warp Terminal subscriptions, members added before cancellation takes effect are billed immediately for the remaining time. For example, adding a member halfway through the final monthly cycle incurs half of that seat's monthly fee. See [member billing](#what-counts-as-a-team-member-and-how-does-billing-work-for-members).

### What happens if I upgrade from monthly to annual billing?

On Build, Max, and Business subscriptions for Warp Terminal, switching to annual billing accounts for the unused portion of the current monthly subscription. You receive credit for the overlapping time rather than paying twice for it; the annual subscription uses its applicable discounted rate. Review the prorated adjustment in checkout or the billing portal before confirming.

The Scale plan for Warp Factories is a monthly team subscription, not an annual per-seat plan.

You can upgrade at any point throughout your subscription on the [Warp upgrade page](https://app.warp.dev/upgrade). You can also open **Manage billing** from your profile menu on that page. See [How do I manage my billing?](#how-do-i-manage-my-billing).

### What happens to unused credits?

Unused included usage does not roll over. Purchased usage rolls over until its 12-month expiration and stays tied to the team that bought it. See [included allowances and existing balances](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/).

### What happens if my payment fails?

If a payment fails, you will receive an email from Stripe and your Warp Team Settings will show a past-due alert. Certain paid plan features and the ability to invite new members will be locked down while your Team is in a past-due state. Paying all unpaid invoices through the billing portal will fully re-enable your paid plan features. See [How do I manage my billing?](#how-do-i-manage-my-billing).

If there are multiple unpaid invoices and you need help resolving them, contact [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev) for assistance.

### What counts as a credit?

A credit is a usage unit, not a token or a fixed number of prompts. Accounts billed in dollars can display usage in credits without changing their billing. Enterprise accounts that bill in credits follow their contracts. See [usage and billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/).

### What counts as an AI token?

Tokens are chunks of text a model reads or generates. Input, output, cache-read, and cache-write tokens can have different prices. Available [usage breakdowns](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/#tracking-usage) show those quantities and costs.

Token limits, such as a model's context window, are separate from your monetary allowance. A token count is not a dollar amount or a credit count.

### How often do my credits reset?

Included usage resets monthly, including on annual subscriptions:

* **Warp Terminal plans** - In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage** for your personal allowance and reset date.
* **Warp Factories** - In the {VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage** for shared balances and the reset date. Personal allowances do not fund factory runs.

Unused included usage does not roll over; [purchased usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/) has a separate 12-month expiration.

### How do credits work for multi-seat teams?

For Warp Terminal, Build, Max, and Business subscriptions have per-seat allowances. New purchases enter a shared team balance. See [included and purchased usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/#included-and-purchased-usage) for spending order.

For Warp Factories, the Scale plan has a shared monthly allowance. Factory runs cannot use a member's included personal allowance. See [factory funding](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#funding-factory-runs).

Admins manage the team's monthly spend limit and auto-reload. See [team usage and purchases](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#tracking-purchases-and-team-usage). Enterprise pools and spending controls follow the [Enterprise agreement](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/).

### How do platform credits factor in?

Platform usage is a separate cost component billed by the agent hour, not another included allowance. Cloud runs incur charges for billable agent time. Local charges depend on the product and plan billed for the run. See [platform billing by product](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/#when-platform-charges-apply) for eligibility, metering, and contract-specific terms.

### What if I need more AI usage than my plan includes?

After included usage runs out, you can:

* **Purchase usage or enable auto-reload** - Add to your available balance on eligible plans. See [purchases and spend limits](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/).
* **Change plans** - Choose a larger included allowance on [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing).
* **Bring your own inference** - Connect an [API key](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/), [custom endpoint](/agents/inference/custom-inference-endpoint/), or supported [subscription](/agents/inference/chatgpt-subscription/). Your provider bills inference separately; applicable [platform charges](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/) still apply.

*BYOK and custom inference endpoint availability is subject to organization size — see [Can I bring my own API key?](#can-i-bring-my-own-api-key) for details.*

For organization-scale needs (cloud-provider managed inference, granular admin controls, or higher seat counts), Enterprise plans include [Bring Your Own LLM (BYOLLM)](/enterprise/enterprise-features/bring-your-own-llm/).

### Can I buy usage on the Free plan without subscribing?

Yes. Users on the Free plan for Warp Terminal can purchase usage without a subscription. See [current usage purchase prices](https://www.warp.dev/pricing).

Use the purchase card on the [Warp upgrade page](https://app.warp.dev/upgrade).

### Who on my team can purchase usage?

On eligible plans, any active team member can make a one-time purchase for the shared team balance, subject to the monthly spend limit. A paid subscription is not required. Admins manage auto-reload, the spend limit, and subscription upgrades.

See [purchasing additional usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/) for the Free-plan web checkout and eligible paid-plan controls.

### Which card is charged when a team member buys usage?

Usage purchases charge the payment method saved for the team, not a separate card for each member. A card saved during the first purchase can become the team's default for later purchases and auto-reloads. See [purchase permissions and payment methods](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#who-can-purchase-usage).

If you see a usage purchase you don't recognize:

* Check **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the Warp app for the team's purchases in the current period.
* Ask a team admin to review the invoices in the billing portal, which lists each purchase and the member who made it. See [How do I manage my billing?](#how-do-i-manage-my-billing).
* Email [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev) if the charge still looks wrong.

### How do I change or remove the card saved for my team?

Team admins change or remove the saved card in the billing portal, reachable from **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the Warp app or from the [Warp Admin Panel](https://app.warp.dev/admin/) on the web. See [How do I manage my billing?](#how-do-i-manage-my-billing) for both paths.

Non-admin members can't open the billing portal. If you added a card to your team through a usage purchase and want it removed, ask a team admin to do it, or email [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev).

Admins can also adjust the team's monthly spend limit in **Settings** > **Billing and usage**. See [spend limits and reset periods](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#configuring-a-monthly-spend-limit).

### How does auto-reload work for teams?

An admin chooses a usage amount and monthly spend limit. Once the applicable included allowance is exhausted and the purchased balance runs low, Warp can buy that amount into the shared team balance. Any member's usage can trigger repeated reloads.

One-time and automatic purchases count toward the same team limit. Reloading stops when the next purchase is blocked by that limit. See [configuring auto-reload](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#2-enable-auto-reload) for controls and reset periods.

### How are cloud agent runs on team plans billed when no individual user triggered them?

Factory runs use [shared team or workspace funding](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#funding-factory-runs), not a member's included personal allowance. For standalone cloud runs, agent API key runs use eligible shared team usage; user-created schedules use the creator's eligible usage by default, or shared team usage when configured to run as a cloud agent.

Review [cloud run billing](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/#billing-for-cloud-agent-runs) and [auto-reload controls](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#2-enable-auto-reload) before relying on unattended runs. Enterprise billing follows your contract.

### Does the Free plan include AI usage?

The Free plan for Warp Terminal uses pay-as-you-go for Warp-provided inference. Check **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the Warp app for any allowance on your account. To use the Warp Agent, you can:

* **Upgrade to a paid plan** - Paid plans include a monthly allowance of Warp-provided AI usage with no extra setup. See [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing).
* **Buy additional usage** - Purchase usage without subscribing. See [Free-plan purchases](#can-i-buy-usage-on-the-free-plan-without-subscribing).
* **Bring your own inference** - Connect an [API key](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/), [ChatGPT subscription](/agents/inference/chatgpt-subscription/), [custom endpoint](/agents/inference/custom-inference-endpoint/), or [SuperGrok subscription](/agents/inference/grok-subscription/). Those requests do not consume Warp-provided inference usage; other eligible run charges still apply.

Without included or purchased usage, or your own inference, conversations on Free cannot run on Warp-provided models. This includes conversations started from prompt suggestions.

### Can I use a Free plan if I'm a developer at a large company or organization?

Yes. Developers at any company size are welcome to use Warp’s Free plan.

If you need access to advanced collaboration features or higher limits, you may benefit from upgrading to a plan, but Warp does not require large organizations to upgrade.

### Are there any Warp discounts for students, non-profits, or open-source teams?

Warp doesn’t currently offer discounts or program credits for students or non-profits. We recommend exploring the Free plan, which includes all core terminal features and is a great starting point for individual or community use.

Warp's client itself is open source under [AGPL v3](https://github.com/warpdotdev/warp/blob/master/LICENSE-AGPL), so open source teams can build, run, and contribute to it directly. See [Contributing to Warp](/support-and-community/community/contributing/) for the flow.

### Where is Warp Drive data for my team stored?

Warp Drive data is securely stored on Google Cloud Platform servers located in the United States. All data is encrypted while being sent and while stored.

For more details, [visit the Security Overview](https://www.warp.dev/legal/security) or contact [security@warp.dev](mailto:security@warp.dev).

### Does Warp have a SOC 2 Type 2 attestation?

Yes. Warp has obtained a SOC 2 Type 2 attestation from an accredited third party. See the [Trust Center](/enterprise/security-and-compliance/trust-center/) to request the report.

### Does Warp have Zero Data Retention policies with LLM providers?

Yes. Warp has provider Zero Data Retention (ZDR) agreements for supported Warp-managed models across plans, with [model-specific exceptions](/agents/inference/model-choice/#models-requiring-provider-retention). See [Privacy and data control](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/#zero-data-retention-zdr) for the policy, including customer-managed inference.

### How can I enable Zero Data Retention in Warp?

Use Warp-managed inference with a model outside the [provider-retention exception list](/agents/inference/model-choice/#models-requiring-provider-retention). Warp's provider ZDR agreements apply to those requests across plans. If you use your own provider account, verify ZDR with that provider. Follow the [Terminal procedure](/agents/inference/model-choice/#reviewing-inference-and-privacy-in-terminal) or [factory ZDR guidance](/factories/zero-data-retention/) to review your settings.

The **Help improve Warp** toggle controls Warp's analytics and content collection for service improvement, not provider ZDR or saved run data. See [Privacy and data control](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/#provider-retention-analytics-and-storage) for these separate controls.

### Can I bring my own API key?

Yes. On eligible plans, you can configure your own OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google API key in **Settings** > **Agents** > **Warp Agent** > **Manage models**. Your provider bills requests routed through that key; they do not consume Warp-provided inference usage.

See [Bring Your Own API Key](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/) for setup steps, the list of supported providers and models, and the differences between BYOK, custom inference endpoints, and BYOLLM.

Personal BYOK is available on the Free and eligible paid plans for Warp Terminal for individuals and organizations with 10 or fewer employees, subject to Warp's Terms of Service. Larger organizations need a Business or Enterprise plan for Warp Terminal. For Warp Factories, use [team-managed keys on the Scale or Enterprise plan](/enterprise/enterprise-features/team-managed-keys-and-endpoints/#plan-eligibility).

For local and cloud charges, see [platform billing by product](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/#when-platform-charges-apply).

### Does Warp support custom inference endpoints?

Yes. Warp can route requests to an OpenAI-compatible inference endpoint, including OpenRouter, LiteLLM, z.ai, or a gateway your team runs. Your endpoint bills inference separately; those requests do not consume Warp-provided inference usage.

See [Custom inference endpoint](/agents/inference/custom-inference-endpoint/) for plan eligibility, configuration steps, billing behavior, and how custom inference endpoints differ from BYOK and Enterprise's managed [Bring Your Own LLM (BYOLLM)](/enterprise/enterprise-features/bring-your-own-llm/) inference.

For personal endpoints, follow the [Warp Terminal and local agent eligibility rules](/agents/inference/custom-inference-endpoint/). For Warp Factories, use [team-managed endpoints on the Scale or Enterprise plan](/enterprise/enterprise-features/team-managed-keys-and-endpoints/#plan-eligibility).

For local and cloud charges, see [platform billing by product](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/#when-platform-charges-apply).

### Does Warp support other model routers or "Bring Your Own LLM"?

With an Enterprise agreement, [AWS Bedrock BYOLLM](/enterprise/enterprise-features/byollm-aws-bedrock/) routes inference through your AWS account and supports factory runs. [Gemini Enterprise BYOLLM (Vertex AI)](/enterprise/enterprise-features/byollm-gemini-enterprise/) routes supported Gemini and Claude models through your Google Cloud project using Workload Identity Federation. It currently supports interactive Warp Agent sessions; cloud runs aren't supported yet.

Custom or in-house model routers outside this list aren't supported by default today. If you have specific requirements, [contact our sales team](https://www.warp.dev/contact-sales).

For lighter-weight routing through any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenRouter, LiteLLM, z.ai, internal gateways), see [Does Warp support custom inference endpoints?](#does-warp-support-custom-inference-endpoints) above.

### What features are available during multi-harness orchestration beta?

For factory runs, selecting [Claude Code](/factories/harnesses/claude-code/) or [Codex](/factories/harnesses/codex/) as a third-party harness requires the Scale or Enterprise plan for Warp Factories. Standalone cloud runs have separate eligibility rules. See [cloud agent harnesses](/factories/harnesses/) for both paths, authentication, and billing.

[Agent Memory](/agents/agent-memory/) is currently in Research Preview. It lets preferences, project knowledge, and learnings from past sessions carry across harnesses and future agent runs. [Contact our sales team](https://www.warp.dev/contact-sales) to request access.

As these features move out of beta or Research Preview, availability, limits, and pricing may change.

### What payment options are available for Warp's self-service plans?

Warp uses Stripe as our payments processor and currently accepts the following payment methods: credit card, debit card, Link, Apple Pay, Google Pay.

We don't support ACH, checks, PayPal, cryptocurrency, or other alternative payment methods at this time.

:::note
To checkout with Apple Pay, use the Safari browser on an Apple device.\
To checkout with Google Pay, use Chrome, make sure you're logged into your Google Wallet account in the browser, and that "Save and fill payment methods" is enabled in the browser settings.

### How do I get a refund?

For monthly subscriptions, we can issue a full refund if you cancel within 24 hours of being charged and no usage has been consumed.

For annual subscriptions, we can issue a full refund within 15 days if no usage has been consumed, or a prorated refund for remaining months after that. See [Warp's refund policy](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#warps-refund-policies) for full details and exceptions.

### Why doesn't my promo code work or why was it disabled?

Warp's promotions are a way to try Warp before committing to a subscription, not get unlimited free or inexpensive AI. To prevent abuse, we're limiting promotion codes to first-time redemptions only, so each account can only use one promotion code.\
\
In addition certain promo codes we issue are only valid for specific plans (not all plans) and expire after a certain time. If you apply a promo code for a certain plan, then upgrade to another plan, the promotion doesn't transfer to the new plan so you will end up paying full price for the upgrade.

:::caution
Warp reserves the right to disable any promotion codes at any time, and cancel the associated subscriptions if we find that the code was being abused.
:::

### How can I subscribe to Warp as tax exempt?

Warp does allow tax exempt organizations to subscribe, but there are some additional steps that you need to take.

1. Create a Warp account, login, and go to the [upgrade page](https://app.warp.dev/upgrade), and select a plan, but **don't checkout or pay yet.** This will only setup your billing account in the system that will be used in the later steps.
2. E-mail [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev) with your proof of your organization tax exempt status and your Warp account email.
3. Warp's team will verify your tax exempt status and will change your account to tax exempt.
4. Finally, subscribe to your plan of choice. You will see no taxes applied to the bill.

### Why can't I subscribe to Warp?

There are certain prohibited and restricted businesses in which Stripe and major credit card networks will not process payments. For the most updated information, see Stripe's [restricted businesses list](https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses).

### I have a question and need help. How can I reach a human at Warp?

The team at Warp is standing by and ready to help. For subscribers technical issues email [support@warp.dev](mailto:support@warp.dev), for subscribers billing issues email [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev), for security concerns email [security@warp.dev](mailto:security@warp.dev), and for questions about privacy email [privacy@warp.dev](mailto:privacy@warp.dev).

### May 2026 pricing changes

The May 21, 2026 update introduced new seat limits, changed how add-on credits are attributed, opened BYOK to every plan, added custom inference endpoints, and began metering platform credits for cloud agent runs. The questions below cover what changed and what to do if any of it affects you.

#### Are there new seat limits per plan?

The May 21, 2026 update introduced seat limits for Warp Terminal plans. See [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing) for current membership limits; those limits do not determine whether a subscription bills per seat. The separate Scale plan for Warp Factories uses a team subscription.

#### What if my team is already above the new seat limit?

Your team's current access doesn't change. Existing members keep their seats and can continue using Warp the same way they do today.

The cap only governs **adding new members**. While your team is at or above the cap, you can't add a new member or backfill a seat after a member leaves. To grow past the cap, switch to the next plan up (or to Enterprise) anytime under **Settings** > **Billing and usage**.

#### How are add-on credits being attributed differently?

Add-on credit attribution has changed twice in 2026:

* **Before May 21, 2026** — Add-on credits on multi-seat teams were pooled; every member drew from a single shared balance.
* **May 21 – August 2026** — Add-on credits were user-scoped; each user had their own balance.
* **As of August 2026** — Add-on credits are **team-pooled again**. Purchases from any member go into the team's shared pool. Remaining user-scoped balances from the May–August window stay attributed to the purchasing member. See the current [spending order](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/#included-and-purchased-usage) and [factory funding rules](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#funding-factory-runs).

On eligible plans, any active team member can purchase usage within the team's monthly spend limit. In the Warp app, admins manage the limit in **Settings** > **Billing and usage**.

For details, see [purchasing additional usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/).

#### What happens to my purchased usage if I leave the team?

Purchased usage stays tied to the team that bought it. Leaving or being removed stops your access; rejoining restores access to any unused, unexpired balance. See [when team membership changes](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#when-team-membership-changes) for details.

#### Can I bring my own API key on the Free plan now?

Yes. The Free plan for Warp Terminal supports personal BYOK, subject to organization-size requirements. See [Bring Your Own API Key](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/) for eligibility, supported providers, and setup steps.

#### What is the new custom inference endpoint feature?

**Custom inference endpoints** let you route Warp's AI traffic through any OpenAI-compatible inference endpoint, including OpenRouter, LiteLLM, z.ai, and internal gateways your team already runs. See [current eligibility](/agents/inference/custom-inference-endpoint/), including organization-size requirements.

Here's how custom inference endpoints differ from BYOK and BYOLLM:

* **BYOK** sends requests through Warp's servers to OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google using your own provider API key.
* **Custom inference endpoint** sends requests to any OpenAI-compatible URL you control or that your team runs.
* **BYOLLM** is managed inference through your cloud provider under an Enterprise agreement. [AWS Bedrock](/enterprise/enterprise-features/byollm-aws-bedrock/) supports factory runs. [Gemini Enterprise (Vertex AI)](/enterprise/enterprise-features/byollm-gemini-enterprise/) supports Gemini and Claude models in your Google Cloud project for interactive sessions; cloud runs aren't supported yet.

For setup and details, see [Custom inference endpoint](/agents/inference/custom-inference-endpoint/).

#### I'm a team admin. What do I need to do?

Review these settings in the Warp app:

* **Check your seat count.** Go to **Settings** > **Teams** to see whether your team is at or above your plan's new seat limit. If you are, see [What if my team is already above the new seat limit?](#what-if-my-team-is-already-above-the-new-seat-limit).
* **Set your team's monthly spend limit** - In **Settings** > **Billing and usage**, configure the limit for one-time purchases and auto-reloads across your team.
* **Explain the shared balance** - New purchases enter the team's shared balance. [Factory runs](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#funding-factory-runs) cannot use members' included personal allowances.
* **Confirm the team's payment method** - Usage purchases charge the team's saved payment method, including purchases by members who aren't admins. Review it in the billing portal. See [which card is charged](#which-card-is-charged-when-a-team-member-buys-add-on-credits).
* **Account for platform usage** - Local runs using customer-supplied inference incur [platform charges](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/) on eligible plans.
