Warp Terminal and agent plans are separate from Warp Factories plans. Use [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing) for current prices and offerings, and [plan guidance](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/) to choose the offering for your work.

Usage covers inference, Warp-hosted compute, and platform services. Your plan determines the included allowance and whether usage is shared or per seat. Your billing terms determine whether charges use dollars or credits.

For provider API keys and custom inference endpoints, see [Inference & providers](/agents/inference/model-choice/).

For Claude organization-linked billing without an API key, see [Managing Claude linked billing](https://docs.warp.dev/agents/inference/claude-platform-linked-billing/).
