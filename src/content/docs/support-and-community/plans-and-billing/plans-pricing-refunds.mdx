Warp Terminal and agent plans are separate from Warp Factories plans. For the latest plans and offerings, see [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing).

## Warp Terminal and agent plans

Choose a Terminal and agent plan for interactive work in Warp. Free, Build, Max, Business, and Enterprise have their own billing terms.

### Existing Warp plans

Build, Max, and Business subscriptions retain their plan-specific per-seat allowances. Check **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the Warp app for your account's allowance. Existing Enterprise contracts retain their agreed pools and terms.

## Warp Factories plans

Choose a factory plan for your team's automated workflows:

* **Start** - For small teams getting started with Warp-hosted factory setup. Start is usage-based, without a monthly shared allowance.
* **Scale** - For teams that need a monthly team subscription with shared included usage, agents such as [Claude Code or Codex](/factories/harnesses/), or a factory-definition repository they own.
* **Enterprise** - For organizations that need custom usage terms, advanced administration, or [customer-hosted infrastructure](/factories/self-hosting/). Your contract sets funding and billing terms.

Hosting your factory definition in your own repository does not move execution or run data to your infrastructure. Enterprise teams configure self-hosted workers and [agent data storage](/factories/self-hosting/data-storage/) separately.

Start with the [Warp Factories quickstart](/factories/quickstart/). For Scale setup, see [SAML SSO](/enterprise/security-and-compliance/sso/) and [team-managed keys and endpoints](/enterprise/enterprise-features/team-managed-keys-and-endpoints/). Third-party harnesses use separate [harness authentication](/factories/harnesses/authentication/).

### Funding factory runs

Factory runs use shared team or workspace usage, including runs started by automations. A member's included personal allowance does not fund factory runs.

On Scale, the shared included allowance covers eligible Warp-provided inference, Warp-hosted compute, and platform usage. After it runs out, runs draw from eligible purchased team or workspace usage. If no usable balance or contracted pay-as-you-go funding remains, runs can be blocked with an [insufficient credits error](/factories/api-and-sdk/troubleshooting/errors/insufficient-credits/).

In the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a>, open **Billing and usage** to check your shared balance. [Add usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/) only if that balance does not cover the work. Enterprise funding follows your contract.

Your provider bills customer-supplied inference separately. That does not remove Warp's [platform charges](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/) or charges for [Warp-hosted compute](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/credits/#compute-usage).

### Upgrading to Scale

In the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a>, open **Billing and usage**, then click **Upgrade to Scale** and **Select plan**. A billing admin completes checkout or confirms the subscription update. If you aren't an admin, ask your Warp admin to upgrade.

New subscriptions and monthly upgrades from Build, Max, and Business use this flow. For an existing Enterprise contract, contact your account team instead.

## Your included allowance

In the factory web app, open **Billing and usage** to check shared balances and the reset date. For personal allowances in the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage**. Existing seats can have a different allowance from new subscriptions, so use the amount shown for your account.

Included usage resets monthly, even when you pay for your subscription annually. Unused included usage does not roll over. Purchased usage is separate: it rolls over until its original 12-month expiration.

If **Billing and usage** shows your allowance and balance in dollars, those amounts are your usage balance. Enterprise contracts that use credits retain their contracted pools, rates, and terms; a dollar cost display does not change those terms. See [Enterprise billing](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/).

## Existing purchased balances

If Warp converts an existing self-serve purchased credit pack to dollar usage, it keeps its face value or equivalent inference value, whichever is higher.

A converted, partially used pack retains the remaining proportion of that value and its original expiration. Check the balance shown in Billing and usage rather than applying a single exchange rate to every credit pack.

## Warp’s refund policies

Review the details of our refund policies below. To request a refund, email [**billing@warp.dev**](mailto:billing@warp.dev) with information about your situation — the more context you provide, the faster we can resolve your request.

:::caution
To request a refund or credit for a product defect, we require your [Conversation ID](/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/sending-us-feedback/#gathering-ai-conversation-id) and [logs](/support-and-community/troubleshooting-and-support/sending-us-feedback/#gathering-warp-logs). You must have **Help improve Warp** enabled, so we can identify the issue before issuing any refund or credit. Learn how to enable it in [Crash reports and telemetry](/support-and-community/privacy-and-security/privacy/#how-to-disable-telemetry-and-crash-reporting).
:::

### Subscription refund policy

**Monthly plans**

If you signed up for a monthly Warp subscription by mistake, we can issue a full refund if you cancel or downgrade within 24 hours of being charged and no usage has been consumed during that billing cycle.

After 24 hours, or if usage has been consumed, we will cancel your subscription so that no future charges occur, and you can continue to use your plan through the end of the current billing cycle.

**Annual plans**

If you signed up for an annual Warp subscription by mistake, we can issue a full refund if you cancel or downgrade within 15 days of being charged and no usage has been consumed during that billing period.

After 15 days, we will refund you for the remaining full months on your subscription, excluding the first month.

### Purchased usage refund policy

* **Unused purchases** - If you bought [additional usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/) by mistake, you can receive a refund as long as none of that purchase has been used. Warp cannot refund a purchase after any of its usage has been consumed. Purchased usage remains available for up to 12 months, including after moving to Free.
* **Unexpected payment method** - Purchases charge the team's saved payment method, and any active team member can make one. See [which card is charged](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/pricing-faqs/#which-card-is-charged-when-a-team-member-buys-add-on-credits) before requesting a refund.

:::caution
Warp does not offer a formal guaranteed uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA). Live incidents can be monitored on our [**Status Page**](https://status.warp.dev/), and open issues are tracked on our [**GitHub Issues**](https://github.com/warpdotdev/Warp/issues).
:::
