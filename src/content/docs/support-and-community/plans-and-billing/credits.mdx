Warp usage pays for model calls, hosted compute, and platform services. Each component has its own rate and meter; a cloud run can incur all three.

<span id="what-are-warp-credits" />

## Dollar usage and credits

Your plan determines the unit Warp uses to charge usage:

* **Dollar billing** - Your usage balance is an amount in US dollars. Usage charges draw from that balance.
* **Credit billing** - Usage is measured and charged in credits. Enterprise contracts that use credits retain their rates and terms.

Changing the display unit doesn't change your billing terms or charges. If you're unsure which unit your account bills in, check your billing terms or contact [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev).

A credit is a usage unit, not a token or a fixed number of prompts.

## Usage rates

Enterprise rates, discounts, margins, and credit conversions follow your contract. [Usage purchase premiums](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#purchasing-usage) affect the amount you pay to buy usage, not the metered rates below.

### Inference rates

For paid self-serve plans billed in dollars, Warp-provided inference is billed at the providers' public API rates. The cost depends on the model's input, output, and cache prices, plus any provider tool charges. Free-plan usage purchases include a purchase premium.

If you use your own [API key](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/) or [inference endpoint](/agents/inference/custom-inference-endpoint/), your provider bills that inference separately. Compute and platform charges are separate from inference.

See [Inference usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/inference-usage/) for how model choice, tokens, caching, and tools affect cost.

### Platform rates

On Free, Build, Max, and Business accounts billed in dollars, platform usage costs **$3 per agent hour**, or **$0.05 per billable minute**. Partial hours are billed proportionally: 30 minutes of billable activity costs $1.50.

An *agent hour* (AH) is one hour of billable agent activity, not a run's elapsed time. Cloud runs, including factory runs and third-party harnesses, incur platform usage. Local runs on Business and Enterprise also incur platform charges when they use customer-supplied inference.

See [Platform usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/) for billable time by harness, eligibility, and exceptions.

### Hosted compute rates

Cloud runs on Warp-hosted compute incur compute charges. Compute cost is the sandbox's rate multiplied by its metered lifetime, not its CPU utilization or billable agent hours. Local runs and self-hosted compute don't incur Warp-hosted compute charges.

The rates below apply to Free, Build, Max, and Business accounts billed in dollars, with no additional compute markup. They are configured compute rates, not a live lookup of a provider's invoice.

Match your [runner's operating system, architecture, and instance shape](/factories/runners/) to the rates below. Available shapes and default sizes depend on your plan.

#### Linux and Windows

Linux x86-64 and aarch64 use the same rate. Windows supports x86-64 and costs twice the Linux rate for the same shape.

To calculate the per-minute rate, take the greater of the vCPU count and half the memory in GB. Multiply that number by $0.0015 for Linux or $0.003 for Windows. For example, 4 vCPUs with 16 GB costs $0.012 per minute on Linux.

Example shapes:

| vCPUs | Memory | Linux per minute | Linux per hour | Windows per minute | Windows per hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 4 GB | $0.003 | $0.18 | $0.006 | $0.36 |
| 4 | 8 GB | $0.006 | $0.36 | $0.012 | $0.72 |
| 8 | 16 GB | $0.012 | $0.72 | $0.024 | $1.44 |
| 16 | 32 GB | $0.024 | $1.44 | $0.048 | $2.88 |

#### macOS

macOS runners use Apple Silicon and have fixed rates for each supported shape:

| vCPUs | Memory | Per minute | Per hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 7 GB | $0.075 | $4.50 |
| 6 | 14 GB | $0.09 | $5.40 |
| 8 | 14 GB | $0.135 | $8.10 |
| 12 | 28 GB | $0.18 | $10.80 |
| 12 | 56 GB | $0.27 | $16.20 |

## Example cloud run

For a Warp Agent run on a self-serve plan billed in dollars, suppose a 4 vCPU / 8 GB Linux sandbox exists for 10 minutes, with 6 billable agent minutes and $0.40 of Warp-provided inference:

* **Compute** - 10 minutes × $0.006 = $0.06.
* **Platform** - 6 minutes × $0.05 = $0.30.
* **Inference** - $0.40.

The run consumes $0.76 from the eligible usage balance. Subscription payments, usage purchase premiums, and bills from your own inference provider are separate.

## Tracking usage

In the Warp app, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage** to check your included allowance, purchased balance, and reset date.

* **Turn usage** - Expand the usage amount below an agent response. Where detailed usage is available, expand a model to see input, output, cache-read, and cache-write tokens, plus web searches and their costs.
* **Conversation usage** - Open the conversation's usage summary to see its total. **View account usage** opens Billing and usage.

The CLI has its own [usage display](/agents/cli/models-and-usage/#usage-and-cost).

### Charges and estimates

Recorded dollar totals represent Warp usage charges, not the total price of your subscription or usage purchases. Older usage can have estimated costs, credit-only details, or no breakdown. An unavailable amount does not mean the run was free. Bills from your own inference provider are not fully represented in Warp's totals.

<span id="credit-limits-and-billing" />

## Included and purchased usage

On self-serve paid plans, included usage is allocated per seat and resets monthly, including on annual subscriptions. Unused included usage does not roll over. Check the allowance shown for your account rather than assuming it matches the amount advertised for a new subscription.

After included usage runs out, Warp draws from available [purchased usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/) on eligible plans. Purchased usage rolls over until its expiration and is shared across the team. Dedicated cloud allowances can be used before your general balance. Enterprise pool sizes and allocation rules follow your contract.

The Free plan uses pay-as-you-go for Warp-provided inference. [Buy additional usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/) without subscribing, [upgrade](https://www.warp.dev/pricing), or [bring your own inference](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/). Separate [platform charges](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/) still apply to eligible runs.

<span id="other-features-that-use-credits" />

## Other metered features

[Generate](/agents/local-agents/generate/) and [AI Autofill in Workflows](/knowledge-and-collaboration/warp-drive/workflows/#ai-autofill) also use inference. Regular shell commands and non-AI terminal features do not consume agent usage.

## Cloud agent runs on team plans

Agent API key runs follow the [team billing rules for cloud runs](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/#billing-for-cloud-agent-runs).

By default, user-created schedules use the creator's eligible included and purchased usage. A schedule configured to run as a cloud agent uses shared team usage instead. Enterprise pools and charges follow your contract.

If no usable balance remains, a run can fail with an [insufficient credits](/factories/api-and-sdk/troubleshooting/errors/insufficient-credits/) error.
