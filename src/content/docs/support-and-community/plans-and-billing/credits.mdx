Warp usage pays for model calls, hosted compute, and platform services. For Warp Terminal and local agents, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the Warp app. For Warp Factories, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the <a href={VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP_URL}>{VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}</a>.

<span id="what-are-warp-credits" />

## Dollar usage and credits

Your plan determines the unit Warp uses to charge usage:

* **Dollar billing** - Your usage balance is an amount in US dollars. Usage charges draw from that balance.
* **Credit billing** - Usage is measured and charged in credits. Enterprise contracts that use credits retain their rates and terms.

Changing the display unit doesn't change your billing terms or charges. If you're unsure which unit your account bills in, check your billing terms or contact [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev).

Usage has three charge components:

* **Inference** - Model calls provided by Warp. If you use your own [API key](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/) or [inference endpoint](/agents/inference/custom-inference-endpoint/), your provider bills that inference separately.
* **Compute** - The sandbox used by an agent on Warp-hosted compute. Local runs and self-hosted compute don't incur this charge.
* **Platform** - Billable agent time for cloud runs and eligible local runs using customer-supplied inference. See [platform usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/#when-platform-charges-apply) for product-specific eligibility and metering.

For paid self-serve plans billed in dollars, Warp-provided inference is priced at public API rates. Compute and platform charges are separate. Enterprise terms follow your contract.

A credit is a usage unit, not a token or a fixed number of prompts.

## Tracking usage

* **Account usage** - Check your balance and reset date in **Settings** > **Billing and usage**.
* **Turn usage** - Expand the usage amount below an agent response. Where detailed usage is available, expand a model to see input, output, cache-read, and cache-write tokens, plus web searches and their costs.
* **Conversation usage** - Open the conversation's usage summary to see its total. **View account usage** opens Billing and usage.

The CLI has its own [usage display](/agents/cli/models-and-usage/#usage-and-cost).

### Who can see team usage

On the Scale plan for Warp Factories, non-admin members see their own usage. Team admins see per-user team usage totals with unlimited history. Their reporting scope follows the [team or workspace they administer](/factories/teams-and-admin-controls/#team-roles-and-factory-access).

Enterprise admin reporting adds user and cost-type breakdowns, including workspace platform usage. See [Enterprise billing](/enterprise/support-and-resources/billing/) for contract-specific controls.

### Charges and estimates

Recorded dollar totals represent Warp usage charges, not the total price of your subscription or usage purchases. Older usage can have estimated costs, credit-only details, or no breakdown. An unavailable amount does not mean the run was free. Bills from your own inference provider are not fully represented in Warp's totals.

## Included and purchased usage

<span id="warp-terminal-and-local-agents" />

<span id="warp-terminal" />

### Warp Agent plans

The Build, Max, and Business plans allocate included usage per seat. Included usage resets monthly, including on annual subscriptions, and does not roll over. Check **Settings** > **Billing and usage** in the Warp app for your allowance rather than assuming it matches a new subscription.

After a member's allowance runs out, eligible runs draw from purchased usage. The Free plan supports [usage purchases](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#warp-terminal-and-local-agents) without subscribing, or [personal inference](/agents/inference/bring-your-own-api-key/).

### Warp Factories

The Scale plan for Warp Factories includes a shared monthly allowance that does not roll over. The Start plan for Warp Factories uses purchased usage without a subscription. Factory runs require shared team or workspace funding, not a member's included personal allowance.

In the {VARS.FACTORY_WEB_APP}, open **Settings** > **Billing and usage** to check shared balances. After shared included usage runs out, factory runs use eligible purchased usage. See [funding factory runs](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#funding-factory-runs) for covered charges, exhaustion behavior, and Enterprise contract terms.

### Purchased balances and contract pools

Purchased usage rolls over until its expiration and is shared across the team. Eligible shared purchases are spent before member-specific purchases usable on the same team. Dedicated cloud allowances can be used before the general balance. Enterprise pool sizes and allocation rules follow your contract.

## How usage is calculated

Inference cost depends on the model's rates and the work required to complete your request:

* **Model choice** - Models have different input, output, and cache prices. A lower-cost model can reduce spending without reducing the number of tokens.
* **Input and output** - Your prompt, conversation history, attached context, and generated response contribute to token usage.
* **Caching** - Cached input can cost less than new input. Cache-read and cache-write tokens are reported separately.
* **Task complexity** - A task can require several model calls, including calls made while using tools or summarizing a long conversation.
* **Provider tools** - Features such as web search can add charges beyond token costs.

Two similar prompts can use different amounts. Use the reported breakdown to compare tasks rather than treating a prompt as a fixed-price unit. See [using tokens efficiently](/guides/configuration/how-to-use-tokens-efficiently-with-ai-coding-agents/) for ways to reduce usage.

## Compute usage

Cloud runs on Warp-hosted compute incur compute charges, whether started from the Warp app, an integration, the CLI, or the {VARS.WARP_PLATFORM_API}. Compute cost is the sandbox's rate multiplied by its metered lifetime, not its CPU utilization or billable agent hours.

Local runs, CI jobs on your own runners, and [self-hosted workers](/factories/self-hosting/) do not incur Warp-hosted compute charges. Platform charges can still apply.

<span id="hosted-compute-rates" />

### Hosted compute pricing and examples

The Start and Scale plans for Warp Factories use passthrough pricing for Warp-hosted compute. Inference and platform usage are billed separately. See [Warp pricing](https://www.warp.dev/pricing) for current plan rates.

Compute rates depend on the runner and hosting configuration. The US-dollar examples below illustrate configured rates for common shapes, not a universal quote for every hosted runner or provider. If your runner's rate is not available, confirm it with [billing@warp.dev](mailto:billing@warp.dev) before estimating spend. Enterprise margins, discounts, and credit conversions follow your contract.

Match the operating system, architecture, and shape to your [runner configuration](/factories/runners/). Available shapes and defaults depend on your plan. [Usage purchase premiums](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/add-on-credits/#purchasing-usage) affect the amount paid to buy usage, not the compute rate.

#### Linux and Windows

In these examples, Linux x86-64 and aarch64 use the same rate. Windows x86-64 costs twice the Linux rate for the same shape.

To calculate these example per-minute rates, take the greater of the vCPU count and half the memory in GB. Multiply by $0.0015 for Linux or $0.003 for Windows. At those rates, 4 vCPUs with 16 GB costs $0.012 per minute on Linux.

Example shapes:

| vCPUs | Memory | Linux per minute | Linux per hour | Windows per minute | Windows per hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 4 GB | $0.003 | $0.18 | $0.006 | $0.36 |
| 4 | 8 GB | $0.006 | $0.36 | $0.012 | $0.72 |
| 8 | 16 GB | $0.012 | $0.72 | $0.024 | $1.44 |
| 16 | 32 GB | $0.024 | $1.44 | $0.048 | $2.88 |

#### macOS

These macOS examples use Apple Silicon with a fixed rate for each listed shape:

| vCPUs | Memory | Per minute | Per hour |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 7 GB | $0.075 | $4.50 |
| 6 | 14 GB | $0.09 | $5.40 |
| 8 | 14 GB | $0.135 | $8.10 |
| 12 | 28 GB | $0.18 | $10.80 |
| 12 | 56 GB | $0.27 | $16.20 |

### Compute metering

For a newly created sandbox, compute is metered from creation to destruction. This includes setup, command execution, and idle time while the sandbox remains running. If a run receives a prewarmed sandbox, that run's metering starts when the sandbox is assigned to it.

Elapsed time is charged proportionally, including partial minutes; it isn't rounded up to whole minutes or hours. Compute can continue after the agent stops responding if the sandbox stays available. Compute charges are recorded when the sandbox is finalized.

## Platform usage

Cloud runs, including factory runs and third-party harnesses, incur platform usage for billable agent time. For Warp Terminal and local agents, Business and Enterprise plans also incur platform charges when local runs use customer-supplied inference. The same local-run rule applies to work billed under a team's Scale plan for Warp Factories. See [product-specific platform billing](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/#when-platform-charges-apply) for other billing contexts and Enterprise contract terms.

See [platform usage](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/platform-credits/) for billable time, exceptions, and the distinction between agent hours and run time.

### Example cloud run

For a dollar-billed Warp Agent run, suppose a 4 vCPU / 8 GB Linux sandbox exists for 10 minutes, with 6 billable agent minutes and $0.40 of Warp-provided inference. Using illustrative rates of $0.006 per compute minute and $3 per agent hour:

* **Compute** - 10 minutes × $0.006 = $0.06.
* **Platform** - 6 minutes × $0.05 = $0.30.
* **Inference** - $0.40.

At those example rates, the run consumes $0.76 from the eligible usage balance. Use the actual compute and platform rates for your configuration when estimating your own run. Subscription payments, usage purchase premiums, and bills from your own inference provider are separate.

## Cloud agent runs on team plans

For factory runs, see [shared funding requirements](/support-and-community/plans-and-billing/plans-pricing-refunds/#funding-factory-runs). The following rules apply to standalone cloud runs.

Agent API key runs follow the [team billing rules for cloud runs](/platform/team-access-billing-and-identity/#billing-for-cloud-agent-runs).

By default, user-created schedules use the creator's eligible included and purchased usage. A schedule configured to run as a cloud agent uses shared team usage instead. Enterprise pools and charges follow your contract.

If no usable balance remains, a run can fail with an [insufficient credits](/factories/api-and-sdk/troubleshooting/errors/insufficient-credits/) error.
