# Refer a Friend

{% hint style="info" %}
If you think you've referred people but have not received an email, contact us at [feedback@warp.dev.](mailto:feedback@warp.dev)&#x20;
{% endhint %}

Open the invite modal from the top-right menu.

{% embed url="https://www.loom.com/share/d4acc97406c9496ab9fff673f975a081" %}
Refer a friend
{% endembed %}

There are two ways you can invite a friend:

1. Send them an invite link. This will redirect them to our product download page.
2. Input their email address which we use to send them an email.

## Reward program

To thank you for spreading the word, we’ll be giving out these rewards:&#x20;

#### 1 invite: An exclusive theme

Once you refer the first person, the referral theme will be available in your theme picker when you restart the app. We will send you an email to alert you.

#### **10 invites: Warp T-shirt**

Once you've referred 10 people, we will send you an email with details on how to claim your Warp T-shirt.

#### 20 invites: An invite to an insiders program

Once you've referred 20 people, you will get an invite to our insiders program. In this program, you will receive private news and early access to features.
